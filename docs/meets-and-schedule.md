Courses, series, meets and seasons, goals from history, the Schedule page and schedule import.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-29 with its wording unchanged; the version tags in headings say when each part was built (the full old file is `docs/archive/CLAUDE-before-split.md`, and version-by-version history is in REQUESTS.md). ## Meets and goals (2.7)
- **Model:** Course (place + checkpoints) → Meet (`S.meets`: `{id, seriesId, courseId, date 'YYYY-MM-DD'|'', time, kind, levels ['V','JV'], season}`) → Race (`race.meetId`, `race.division`: GV, BV, GJV, BJV, OPEN). `S.series` = `[{id, name}]` is what links a meet across years; renaming a series never breaks a link. Season = Aug 1 to Jul 31 (`seasonOf()`, `meetSeason()`; undated meets keep `season`). All synced (kinds `series`, `meets`) with soft delete; trash kinds `meet`, `series`.
- **Year to year** goes by `seriesId` + `division` (+ `courseId` for "Last time on this course"), never by typed names. Saved results (`raceModel()`) carry `meetId`, `seriesId`, `division`; an older race gets them from a link edit (`{op:'link', …}` in `h.edits`, applied by `modelOf()`; Undo appends an unlink).
- **Race setup:** `newRace()` picks today's meet, else the next one in the same season (`defaultMeet()`); `applyMeet()` loads its course and checkpoints and names the race; the division buttons (`divsOf()`, `applyDivision()`) preselect runners only when none are picked: who ran that division at the last meet (`preselectFor()`), else for a first race the runners marked Girls/Boys. The name follows meet + division until typed (`autoName()`, `nameAuto`).
- **Goals** (`fillGoals()` / `goalFor()`): Compare to = Season best (default), PR, Last race at this distance, Last time on this course, Last year at this meet, Custom, None. Each runner gets the chosen source, else season best, else PR, else blank, with `goalTag` SB / PR / Last / Course / Meet; typing a goal sets Custom. History = Team history + races on this phone, corrections applied, soft-deleted entries skipped (`pastRaces()`). Grid order uses the goal, else the PR (`sortByGoal()`).
- **Girls/Boys:** `athlete.gender` 'G'/'B' (blank = unknown, left out of Firestore). Team tab G/B button cycles – → G → B; Paste a list takes a third column (Girls/Boys/G/B); Add runner has Girls/Boys buttons.
- **Meets screen** (`meetsSheet()`, from Settings > Meets and the Meets button in race setup): by season; tap a meet to edit (`meetEditSheet()`: series or new series, rename series, course or new course, date, time, kind, levels, Delete with Undo). "Load the 2026 schedule" (`seed2026()`, `SEED_2026`: fixed ids `c-…`, `s-…`, `m26-…`, so two coaches loading it at once still get one schedule; an existing course with the same name is reused). "Start a new season" (`newSeason()`: confirm + snapshot, copies the latest season's meets with blank dates). "Link past races" (`linkSheet()`: saved races without a meet, suggested by date).
- **Meet days:** `tests/meetday.sh` still lists the 2026 dates; keep it in step with the Meets screen.

## Schedule (3.5; the Boys colour is in docs/ui.md)
- **Schedule page** (`scheduleSheet(y)`; `meetsSheet()` is an alias kept for old callers): one season at a time (`schedSeason`, a season picker; this season first), "+ Add meet" at the top (`[data-mx=add]`), each meet a `.swipe` row (`.sched-sw[data-mid]`: leading Edit `[data-ms=edit]`; trailing Not attending / Attending `[data-ms=att]` and Delete `[data-ms=del]`), tap = edit (`[data-meet-ed]`). Below: Import schedule (`[data-mx=import]`), Start a new season (`[data-mx=season]`), Load the 2026 schedule (`[data-mx=seed]`, only without a 2026 season), Link past races. Reached from Data > Meets (`#openSched`, with "Next: meet · date" in `#schedNext`), race setup ("Schedule", `[data-ra=meets]`) and Settings > Data > Schedule (`#openMeets`).
- **Not attending this year:** `meet.notAttending: true` (left out when false; synced, rules allow the bool). `defaultMeet()` and `schedule()` skip it, so race setup, Data's "Next" and the Workouts goal line move to the next meet; the meet keeps its series, course and links to past years, and race setup still lists it "(not attending)". `setAttending()` toggles with an Undo bar; the meet editor has the same switch. `applyRemote` drops the field when another phone sends the meet without it. Start a new season copies meets as attending. `brillionOff()` marks Brillion 10/8/2026 once per phone (`S.settings.brillionNA350`, only until that day, 4 s after load so team sync has the schedule).
- **Delete** (`deleteMeet()`): trash + Undo bar, from the swipe and the editor.
- **Edits show everywhere:** saving a meet clears `memo` and calls `refreshAll()`; the official-results and combined-race caches key on meet dates and courses.
- **Import schedule** (`parseCSVText()`, `parseScheduleCSV()`, `schedImportPreview()`, `saveScheduleImport()`): header row with date and meet (levels, location, start time optional; other columns ignored; BOM, quotes, CRLF), dates YYYY-MM-DD or M/D/YYYY (no year = next fall), levels default JV + V. Series: same name (`meetKey`) or a known alias (`knownSeries`) → "matched: check", else New series; course by location name, else New course; a picker per row to change either. Meets already on the schedule (same series and date) are skipped. Snapshot first; one Undo puts the meets and the series and courses it made into Recently deleted. Template: `docs/schedule-template.csv`.
- Tests: `tests/e2e31.js`; rules: `notAttending` cases in `rules.test.mjs` (342).
