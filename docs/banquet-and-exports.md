# Banquet book, social graphics and the coach's workbook (3.19)

Data > Highlights > **Share** (the third mode beside Latest meet and Season) and Settings > Data > **Export** open the same three sheets, for the season picked in the nav bar (`ssSeason()`). All in app.js "banquet book, social graphics and the coach's workbook (3.19)", right after the season and awards section; the spreadsheet writer is `xlsx.js` (`window.MSXlsx`, loaded before app.js, in the service worker's `SHELL`); runner photos also in sync.js "runner photos (3.19)" and firestore.rules `photoOk()` and `match /photos/{aid}`. See also: `docs/season-and-awards.md` (season stories, team cards, award recipients), `docs/data-and-charts.md` (latest-meet highlights, race-day ratings, `teamRows()`), `docs/weather.md` (weather and its credit), `docs/training-and-paces.md` (the pace chart).

## What never goes in an export
Celebrations and results only. Never: a next step or next target, growth focus, award candidates, rankings, math or evidence, Captain, good standing, varsity status or a race's Varsity/JV level, a coach note, a context-tag note, or a health reason (a comeback is "strong return to racing"; a tagged result in the workbook says only "Left out of trends: Yes"). The awards page of the banquet book lists only the recipients the coaches marked (`awRecip()`), and only when "Include awards" is on. `tests/e2e48.js` checks every book page, graphic and workbook string against a list of coach-only words, with an Injury-tagged race and a note in the data.

## Text that always fits
Every line on a canvas is drawn by `tbox(x, text, X, Y, W, o)`: at its size if it fits, else smaller down to `o.min`, else onto more lines (up to `o.lines`, within the height `o.h`), else smaller still and onto as many lines as it takes. Text is never cut and never runs past its box. With `GLOG` set (tests), every line drawn is logged with its box; e2e48 checks every line fits and that the lines together are the whole text, for every name length from 1 to 60 characters.

## Banquet book (PDF)
- `bookSheet()`: Team (Girls / Boys / Both), Content (Celebrations / Full stats), Include awards (says when no recipient is marked yet), Include middle school history; remembered per phone in `S.settings.book` (`BOOK_DEF`). A page estimate, then **Make the banquet book** (progress "n of N") and **Share the PDF** (a second tap, so iOS still allows the share sheet after a long build).
- `bookPages(Y, o, prog)` draws one canvas at a time (1650 × 1275, landscape letter at 150 dpi) and turns each into a JPEG straight away; `bookPdf()` = `pdfPages(pages, {full:true})` (every image edge to edge on a 792 × 612 page).
- Pages: **cover** (`bkCover`: logo, school, Girls/Boys/Cross country, the season, runners, meets, PRs, season bests, miles raced); **team pages** (`bkTeam`: top-5 average and 1–5 spread over the season with the best and tightest meet, PRs, season bests, a chart of the top-5 average at each meet, the roster: alphabetical, or by season best with numbers in Full stats); **meet pages** (`bkMeetPages`, more than one when the list is long: weather with its flags and the Open-Meteo credit, each team's numbers and highlights that day, each runner's time with their highlight badges; Full stats adds pace and place on the team); **runner pages** (`bkRunner`: photo or initials, the season story, season best, PR, opener → best, vs last season, team place, a chart of the season's races, milestones and highlights, season bests by season, middle school seasons when asked; Celebrations shows vs last season only when faster and team place only when it went up; Full stats shows both and every race); **awards** (`bkAwards`, recipients only); **the season by the numbers** (`bkClosing`: PRs, season bests, miles raced, races, meets, runners, the biggest improvements from opener to season best, each team's fastest top-5 average and tightest spread).
- Team colours: navy `#13294b` and Carolina blue `#4b9cd3`, Girls `#b8247f`, Boys `#0a6e94` (`TEAMC`); the logo is `icons/icon-512.png` on a white disc (`logoBadge`). Big numbers in Barlow Condensed (`CLOCKF`, loaded before drawing by `exFonts()`).

## Social graphics (PNG)
- `gfxSheet()`: Graphic (Meet recap, PR spotlight, Team milestone, Season wrap), what it's about (a meet, a PR, a milestone: `gfxChoices()`), Team, Size (Square 1080 × 1080, Story 1080 × 1920), Names (Full, First, Initials: `S.settings.gfxNames`, remembered). The preview is drawn first; **Share the image** is enabled once the PNG is ready (so the share sheet opens from the tap).
- `gfxDraw()`: navy with diagonal stripes, the logo and school at the top, celebrations only. Story keeps the top and bottom 250 px clear (Instagram's controls). Meet recap: the meet, each team's PRs, SBs and top-5 average, then one row per runner with a highlight badge and their time, "+ n more" when they don't fit. PR spotlight: NEW PR, the name, the time, how much faster, the meet. Team milestone: fastest top-5 average, tightest spread, PRs this season, PRs at one meet (3 or more), milestone breakthroughs. Season wrap: PRs, season bests, miles raced, meets, the biggest improvements.
- Photos are never used in graphics.

## Coach's workbook (.xlsx)
- `wbSheet()` → `wbBlob(Y)` → `MSXlsx.build({styles: WB_STYLES, dxfs: WB_DXF, sheets: wbSheets(Y)})` → **Share the workbook**.
- Sheets: **Season board** (one row per runner, Girls then Boys by season best: grade, distance, season best, PR, average, course- and weather-adjusted average, opener → best and vs last season in seconds with colour scales, team rank, meets, PRs, SBs; frozen header and name, filters); **Meet grid** (runners down, 5K race days across with meet / date / course header rows, a season-average formula per runner, each time green when more than 1% faster than that average and red when more than 1% slower); **Splits** (every checkpoint: elapsed, split, segment, pace per mile, who timed it); **Progress** (5K season bests by season, the last six, a "vs the season before" formula, an Excel sparkline per runner, the best season green); **Team** (top-5 average, 1–5 spread and runners per meet for Girls and Boys, season-average formulas); **Training paces** (every effort at 200 to the mile per runner, Easy as a fast and a slow row); **Weather** (the numbers and flags per meet, with the CC BY credit); **Raw data** (every result, every season, high school and middle school, official, hand-timed and typed, one row each).
- Times are time values (a fraction of a day) with `[m]:ss.0` / `[m]:ss`; differences are seconds with `+0.0" s"`; dates are dates. Formulas are written with their values, and `fullCalcOnLoad` recalculates them on open.
- `xlsx.js` writes the parts in the schema's order (an out-of-order part is what makes Excel offer a repair), shared strings, one cell format per named style, frozen panes, filters with their `_xlnm._FilterDatabase` name, merged cells, colour scales and formula rules, and x14 sparklines in the sheet's `extLst`. The zip is deflated with `CompressionStream('deflate-raw')` where the browser has it, stored otherwise.
- Numbers shows the times as durations (an average with milliseconds) and doesn't draw Excel sparklines; the colours still show the trend. Google Sheets imports .xlsx the same way (not testable on the Mac).

## Runner photos
- One per runner, for the banquet book only: a runner's profile > Edit (`resultSheet`) has Add a photo / Change photo / Remove (`photoFieldHTML`, `bindPhotoField`). The picked image is scaled to at most 600 px on its long side as a JPEG (quality from 0.82 down until it's at most 300,000 characters as a data URL; `phFromFile`).
- Kept in their own IndexedDB database **`mustang-splits-photos`** (store `photos`, key `aid`), never in the `mustang-splits:v1` entry and never in the main `mustang-splits` database (so older versions, which open that one at version 2, are untouched). `PHOTOS` (a Map) is loaded at start (`PHOTOS_READY`).
- Data safety: Change puts the old photo in Recently deleted ("photo (replaced)") with an Undo; Remove is a soft delete (`deleted`, content kept) with a copy in the trash and an Undo; Recently deleted restores either (`restoreKind` case `photo` → `phRestore`). Delete permanently removes the runner's photo here and on the team. Back up includes the photos beside the live data (`photos: [...]`); Restore adds photos the phone doesn't have and never removes one. Snapshots don't include photos (they're never changed in bulk).
- Team (sync.js): `teams/{t}/photos/{runner id}` = `{aid, data, w, h, at, byName, deleted…, updatedAt, updatedBy}`. `savePhoto()` writes one; `photoDeleted()` soft-deletes or restores; the listener hands every document to `MSApp.photosRemote()` (a newer one replaces this phone's copy; a change not sent yet stays), and this phone's unsent photos go up once per app open. Before the 3.19 rules are published the team refuses these quietly (`quietKinds` 'photos'): each phone keeps its photos to itself.
- **Rules (3.19.0):** members read; create and update with `photoOk(aid)` (only those fields, the document id is the runner, a JPEG data URL of 100 to 300,000 characters, sizes 1–2000, `at` a number, the writer's own uid and server time) or a soft delete / restore of the flags; delete only by an admin with that runner's purge record.
