# Banquet book, social graphics and the coach's workbook (3.19; the book as a portfolio and one style with logos in 3.22)

Data > Highlights > **Share** (the third mode beside Latest meet and Season) and Settings > Data > **Export** open the same three sheets, for the season picked in the nav bar (`ssSeason()`). All in app.js "banquet book, social graphics and the coach's workbook (3.19)", right after the season and awards section; the spreadsheet writer is `xlsx.js` (`window.MSXlsx`, loaded before app.js, in the service worker's `SHELL`); runner photos also in sync.js "runner photos (3.19)" and firestore.rules `photoOk()` and `match /photos/{aid}`. See also: `docs/season-and-awards.md` (season stories, team cards, award recipients), `docs/data-and-charts.md` (latest-meet highlights, race-day ratings, `teamRows()`), `docs/weather.md` (weather and its credit), `docs/training-and-paces.md` (the pace chart).

## What never goes in an export
Celebrations and results only. Never: a next step or next target, growth focus, award candidates, rankings, math or evidence, Captain, good standing, varsity status or a race's Varsity/JV level, a coach note, a context-tag note, or a health reason (a comeback is "strong return to racing"; a tagged result in the workbook says only "Left out of trends: Yes"). The awards page of the banquet book lists only the recipients the coaches marked (`awRecip()`), and only when "Include awards" is on. `tests/e2e48.js` checks every book page, graphic and workbook string against a list of coach-only words, with an Injury-tagged race and a note in the data.

## Text that always fits
Every line on a canvas is drawn by `tbox(x, text, X, Y, W, o)`: at its size if it fits, else smaller down to `o.min`, else onto more lines (up to `o.lines`, within the height `o.h`), else smaller still and onto as many lines as it takes. Text is never cut and never runs past its box. With `GLOG` set (tests), every line drawn is logged with its box; e2e48 checks every line fits and that the lines together are the whole text, for every name length from 1 to 60 characters.

## One style for the graphics and the book (3.22)
- `exStyle()` = `{look, accent, bgImg, names}` from `S.settings.gfxLook` (Classic navy / Sky / White / Dark), `gfxAccent` (Carolina blue / Sky / White), `gfxBgImg` (none / LC logo watermark / Mustang logo watermark) and `gfxNames` (3.19). `exStyleSet(k, v)` remembers a choice and still writes `gfxBg` as `'stripes'` or `'light'` for a rolled-back phone. `exTheme(style, teams)`: the palette (`EX_PAL`: background, ink, sub, card, rule, gold, the accent for this look) and `paint(x, W, H, watermark)`; `teamOn(g)` is the Girls / Boys colour readable on that look. **Contrast:** every look's ink and sub colour is at least 4.5:1 on its background and every accent at least 3:1; an accent that can't be (white on White or Sky) is shown as navy, and the swatch shows what you get (`MSApp.exContrast(look, accent)`, checked for every pair by `tests/e2e51.js`). The rows (`styleRowsHTML(st, withNames)`, `bindStyleRows`) sit in the graphics sheet and the book sheet; the logo backgrounds are off (saying where to add the logo) until that logo exists.
- **Logos** (`LOGO_KINDS`: `lc`, `mustang`) come from files the coach adds in Settings > Data > Export > **Logos** (`logosSheet()`): never in the repo, never sent to the team. Kept in their own IndexedDB database **`mustang-splits-logos`** (store `logos`, key `kind`; `LOGOS`, `LOGOS_READY`) as PNG with transparency (`lgFromFile`: at most 1024 px on the long side; an image with no transparent pixel has its white background cleared, soft at the edge) and in backups (`logos: [...]`; Restore adds the ones the phone doesn't have). Change keeps the old one in Recently deleted ("(replaced)"); Remove is a soft delete with an Undo (`restoreKind` case `logo` → `lgRestore`). `teamLogo()` = the Mustang logo, else the LC logo, else the app's icon: the badge at the top of every graphic and page; the watermark (`exWatermark(st)`) is drawn faint (alpha 0.08 light / 0.11 dark) behind the content. The untracked `mustang-splits logo.png` in the repo folder is still left alone (the coach adds his files on the phone or restores a backup that holds them).

## The share studio, palettes, backgrounds and the bundled logos (3.23)
- **Logos** come with the app (`brand/`, built from the two source files by `tests/tools/make-brand.py`: `lc-logo.png` 2000 px master plus 1x/2x/3x, trimmed, transparent; `mustang-navy/carolina/sky/white/knockout.svg`, the vectors kept, the viewBox trimmed to the horse) and are in the service worker's `SHELL`. `lgImg(kind, variant)` returns the coach's own file when one was added, else the bundled mark (`BRAND`); `teamLogo()` is the LC monogram. Settings > **Display > Team logos** (`logosSheet()`): Replace with a file, "Use the bundled one" (a soft remove with Undo); a replacement lives in `mustang-splits-logos` and in backups as before. 3.22's design required adding files in Settings > Data > Export > Logos and shipped none, so nothing showed: the header fell back to the app icon and the watermark choices were disabled.
- **Palettes** (`EX_PALS`: Navy & Sky, Sky & Navy, White & Navy, Midnight) and **backgrounds** (`EX_BGK`: Solid, Gradient (navy to a deep Carolina), Diagonal Stripes, LC Watermark (large, off the bottom left, 11–14%), Mustang Watermark (large, off the right edge, 12–16%), LC Pattern (tiled monograms at 6%), Photo Duotone (the Photo design only: navy / sky duotone with a navy band for the words)). `exStyle()` = `{pal, bg, names, design}` in `S.settings.gfxPal / gfxBgKind / gfxNames / gfxDesign`; `exStyleSet()` still writes the 3.22 and 3.19.1 fields for a rolled-back phone. **Contrast:** `exUnder(P, bg)` lists the darkest and lightest colour text can sit on for a background (watermarks and patterns at their strongest); `exOk(pal, bg)` requires ink 4.5:1 and the accent and both team colours 3:1 on all of them; `gfxCombos()` is what the studio and the book offer. A failing pair is hidden, never shown.
- **Typography** (`tbox`): every line goes through `typo()` (curly quotes and apostrophes, en dashes in number ranges, em dashes, a true minus before a number, single spaces); a line with `role:'h'` is set in AP Title Case (`apTitle()`: articles, short conjunctions and prepositions lowercase inside a title; all-caps words, numbers and names with inner capitals kept). No single-word last line when the line above can spare a word. Dates in exports are `fmtDay2()` ("Sat, Oct 31"); times in book tables are `fmtT1()` (always one decimal, so right-aligned tabular columns align). `GLOG` lines carry `role`, the nominal size `sz` and the face (`display` / `ui`) for the lint.
- **The studio** (`gfxSheet()`): the title menu (design · subject, `[data-x=pick]` opens the Design and subject selects and Team), the live preview with Square / Story under it, **Designs** (a swipeable gallery of the templates rendered with the current data, `gfxThumb`), **Colors** (four palette swatches), **Background** (thumbnails, `bgThumb`; only the combinations that pass; Photo Duotone only for the Photo design), **Names** (Full / First / Initials), the suggested caption (`gfxCaption()`: celebrations only, with Copy) and Share pinned at the bottom. No label is truncated (grid rows wrap); no segmented control has more than three options.
- **Templates** (`gfxDraw(Y, o, L, A)`, `o.design`): Meet Recap (every runner who raced, badges when they have one), Results Board (ranked, the fastest ten at most, never cramped, then "+ n more Mustangs raced"), Spotlight (one runner's latest race: name, time, distance and pace, place, badges, the top highlight), Milestone (one big number), Editorial (always White & Navy and solid: thin sky rules, a small LC monogram), Photo (the runner's photo in duotone, name and time on the band; offered only when a photo exists), Season Wrap. Three nominal sizes per graphic besides the brand lockup (`sc`: a hero number, display, body, label); names and times in the condensed display face; the story keeps the top and bottom 250 px clear. 3.19's kinds `pr` and `team` map to Spotlight and Milestone (`LEGACY_KIND`).

## Banquet book (PDF)
- **3.22: two formats** (`bkGeo(fmt)`): **Print** 1275 × 1650 (8.5 × 11 in at 150 dpi) with every line of text inside a half-inch safe margin (`G.m` = 75 px; the background and watermark are full bleed), and **Screen** 1920 × 1080 (16:9, for the banquet projector; its PDF pages are 960 × 540 pt, `pdfPages(P, {full, page})`). Options (`S.settings.book`): `fmt`, and `only` (a runner id: one certificate page; "Share the PDF" and "Share as an image (PNG)"). The sheet (`bookSheet()`) has Format, Pages (whole book or one runner), Team, Content, awards, middle school, the style rows, and a live preview (`bookPreview()`: the cover, or the chosen runner's certificate).
- **Pages** (`bookPages(Y, o, prog)`; each function takes the geometry `G` and the theme `T`): cover (`bkCover`), team pages (`bkTeam`: tiles, the top-5 chart, the roster; on screen the roster sits beside the chart), meet pages (`bkMeetPages`), **a certificate per runner** (`bkRunner`: the photo or initials with a team-colour frame, the team logo, name, grade and team, the season story, up to four tiles, **Season in review** (this season's races, and the season best by season with the PR in gold when there is more than one season), **Courses and times** (`exCourseRows(s)`: each course raced this season, the best time there, PR / SB / Course best badges only when the numbers say so), **Biggest achievements** (the season badges and phrases, PRs, the milestone), middle school history when asked, and signature lines (Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Date)), **course pages** (`bkCourse`, from `exCourses(Y, teams)`: each course raced this season with its meets, per team the course record (the fastest Mustang ever there at the course's main distance, from every saved race and official list, `runnerG` for the team) and the best this season, and every result there this season), awards (`bkAwards`, recipients only, when asked), the season by the numbers (`bkClosing`).
- Names in the book are always full names (it's for the banquet); the style's names choice is for the graphics.
- **3.23 polish:** every title and subtitle in AP Title Case (`role:'h'`), smart typography everywhere (see above), one date format, one-decimal tabular times, no widows; the running header and page number on every page except the cover and the certificates; **certificates** (`bkCertFrame`) have a fine double border inside the safe margins, the LC monogram with the school and season, "Season Certificate", the Mustang mark as a seal top right, the photo (or initials), labeled signature lines (Head Coach, Assistant Coach) and a Date line. The book sheet flags photos under 360 × 450 px as likely to print soft. The book never uses the Photo Duotone background.
- `bookSheet()` (3.19 options, still there): Team (Girls / Boys / Both), Content (Celebrations / Full stats), Include awards (says when no recipient is marked yet), Include middle school history; remembered per phone in `S.settings.book` (`BOOK_DEF`). A page estimate, then **Make the banquet book** (progress "n of N") and **Share the PDF** (a second tap, so iOS still allows the share sheet after a long build).
- `bookPages(Y, o, prog)` draws one canvas at a time and turns each into a JPEG straight away (3.19 drew 1650 × 1275 landscape pages; 3.22 draws the print or screen size above); `bookPdf()` = `pdfPages(pages, {full:true, page})` (every image edge to edge).
- Pages: **cover** (`bkCover`: logo, school, Girls/Boys/Cross country, the season, runners, meets, PRs, season bests, miles raced); **team pages** (`bkTeam`: top-5 average and 1–5 spread over the season with the best and tightest meet, PRs, season bests, a chart of the top-5 average at each meet, the roster: alphabetical, or by season best with numbers in Full stats); **meet pages** (`bkMeetPages`, more than one when the list is long: weather with its flags and the Open-Meteo credit, each team's numbers and highlights that day, each runner's time with their highlight badges; Full stats adds pace and place on the team); **runner pages** (`bkRunner`: photo or initials, the season story, season best, PR, opener → best, vs last season, team place, a chart of the season's races, milestones and highlights, season bests by season, middle school seasons when asked; Celebrations shows vs last season only when faster and team place only when it went up; Full stats shows both and every race); **awards** (`bkAwards`, recipients only); **the season by the numbers** (`bkClosing`: PRs, season bests, miles raced, races, meets, runners, the biggest improvements from opener to season best, each team's fastest top-5 average and tightest spread).
- Team colours: navy `#13294b` and Carolina blue `#4b9cd3`, Girls `#b8247f`, Boys `#0a6e94` (`TEAMC`); the logo is `icons/icon-512.png` on a white disc (`logoBadge`). Big numbers in Barlow Condensed (`CLOCKF`, loaded before drawing by `exFonts()`).

## Social graphics (PNG)
- `gfxSheet()`: Graphic (Meet recap, PR spotlight, Team milestone, Season wrap), what it's about (a meet, a PR, a milestone: `gfxChoices()`), Team, Size (Square 1080 × 1080, Story 1080 × 1920), then the style rows (3.22: Look, Background, Accent, Names; see above; 3.19.1's six background swatches are gone, `gfxBg` is only read for a first default). The preview is drawn first; **Share the image** is enabled once the PNG is ready (so the share sheet opens from the tap).
- `gfxDraw(Y, o, L, wm)`: the look's background with the watermark, the team logo, the school and "Cross Country · season" (Title Case, 3.19.1) at the top, celebrations only. Tests may still pass `o.bg` (a 3.19.1 background name, mapped to a look) or `o.style`.
- Backgrounds before 3.22 (`GFX_BG`, `gfxTheme(k, teams)`, kept in the code for reference): Navy stripes, Navy, Carolina blue, Team colour, Navy and blue, Light. 3.22 replaced them with the looks above.
- **Meet recap lists every runner who raced that day** (3.21), Girls then Boys in finishing order, with a highlight badge only when they have one (3.19–3.20 listed runners with a badge only, so a runner without one was left off and a one-runner meet said "1 Mustangs raced."). One runner gets a spotlight (`gfxSpot()`: name, time, distance and pace, overall place when official, team place when there is a team, their badges, the top highlight's sentence); two or three runners get tall rows (`gfxRows()` `big`).
- Names in lists (`gfxRows()`): every name if at all possible. One column with rows as tall as there's room for, then two columns (smaller badges), and "+ n more" only when even two columns at their smallest can't hold everyone. Every name in a list is the same size: the largest at which they all fit on one line, down to 70% of the row's size; a name still too long wraps onto a second line at that size. The square season wrap puts its four numbers in one row so more names fit. Story keeps the top and bottom 250 px clear (Instagram's controls). Meet recap: the meet, each team's PRs, SBs and top-5 average, then one row per runner with a highlight badge and their time, "+ n more" when they don't fit. PR spotlight: NEW PR, the name, the time, how much faster, the meet. Team milestone: fastest top-5 average, tightest spread, PRs this season, PRs at one meet (3 or more), milestone breakthroughs. Season wrap: PRs, season bests, miles raced, meets, the biggest improvements (as many as fit).
- Photos are never used in graphics.
- **Photos on the Team tab (3.22):** ⋯ › **Add photos** (`photoRunSheet()`) walks the runners without a photo one after another: Take a photo (`<input capture="environment">`, the camera at practice) or Choose from the camera roll, Skip; the count ("4 of 14 runners have a photo"). A runner's Edit sheet has Take a photo beside Add a photo (`[data-ph=cam]` / `[data-ph=camfile]`).

## Coach's workbook (.xlsx)
- `wbSheet()` → `wbBlob(Y)` → `MSXlsx.build({styles: WB_STYLES, dxfs: WB_DXF, sheets: wbSheets(Y)})` → **Share the workbook**.
- Sheets: **Season board** (one row per runner, Girls then Boys by season best: grade, distance, season best, PR, average, course- and weather-adjusted average, opener → best and vs last season in seconds with colour scales, team rank, meets, PRs, SBs; frozen header and name, filters); **Meet grid** (runners down, 5K race days across with meet / date / course header rows, a season-average formula per runner, each time green when more than 1% faster than that average and red when more than 1% slower); **Splits** (every checkpoint: elapsed, split, segment, pace per mile, who timed it); **Progress** (5K season bests by season, the last six, a "vs the season before" formula, an Excel sparkline per runner, the best season green); **Team** (top-5 average, 1–5 spread and runners per meet for Girls and Boys, season-average formulas); **Training paces** (every effort at 200 to the mile per runner, Easy as a fast and a slow row); **Weather** (the numbers and flags per meet, with the CC BY credit); **Raw data** (every result, every season, high school and middle school, official, hand-timed and typed, one row each).
- Times are time values (a fraction of a day) with `[m]:ss.0` / `[m]:ss`; differences are seconds with `+0.0" s"`; dates are dates. Formulas are written with their values, and `fullCalcOnLoad` recalculates them on open.
- `xlsx.js` writes the parts in the schema's order (an out-of-order part is what makes Excel offer a repair), shared strings, one cell format per named style, frozen panes, filters with their `_xlnm._FilterDatabase` name, merged cells, colour scales and formula rules, and x14 sparklines in the sheet's `extLst`. The zip is deflated with `CompressionStream('deflate-raw')` where the browser has it, stored otherwise.
- Numbers shows the times as durations (an average with milliseconds) and doesn't draw Excel sparklines; the colours still show the trend. Google Sheets imports .xlsx the same way (not testable on the Mac).

## Runner photos
- One per runner, for the banquet book only: a runner's profile > Edit (`resultSheet`) has Add a photo / Change photo / Remove (`photoFieldHTML`, `bindPhotoField`). The picked image is scaled to at most 600 px on its long side as a JPEG (quality from 0.82 down until it's at most 300,000 characters as a data URL; `phFromFile`).
- Kept in their own IndexedDB database **`mustang-splits-photos`** (store `photos`, key `aid`), never in the `mustang-splits:v1` entry and never in the main `mustang-splits` database (so older versions, which open that one at version 2, are untouched). `PHOTOS` (a Map) is loaded at start (`PHOTOS_READY`).
- Data safety: Change puts the old photo in Recently deleted ("photo (replaced)") with an Undo; Remove is a soft delete (`deleted`, content kept) with a copy in the trash and an Undo; Recently deleted restores either (`restoreKind` case `photo` → `phRestore`). Delete permanently removes the runner's photo here and on the team. Back up includes the photos beside the live data (`photos: [...]`); Restore adds photos the phone doesn't have and never removes one. Snapshots don't include photos (they're never changed in bulk).
- Team (sync.js): `teams/{t}/photos/{runner id}` = `{aid, data, w, h, at, byName, deleted…, updatedAt, updatedBy}`. `savePhoto()` writes one; `photoDeleted()` soft-deletes or restores; the listener hands every document to `MSApp.photosRemote()` (a newer one replaces this phone's copy; a change not sent yet stays), and this phone's unsent photos go up once per app open. Before the 3.19 rules are published the team refuses these quietly (`quietKinds` 'photos'): each phone keeps its photos to itself.
- **Rules (3.19.0):** members read; create and update with `photoOk(aid)` (only those fields, the document id is the runner, a JPEG data URL of 100 to 300,000 characters, sizes 1–2000, `at` a number, the writer's own uid and server time) or a soft delete / restore of the flags; delete only by an admin with that runner's purge record.
