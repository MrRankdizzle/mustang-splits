# Stopwatches

The Stopwatches tab: rows and their action button, sharing with other coaches, splitting groups, Undo, End workout, the lap list, + New and the first-run UI.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-29 with its wording unchanged; the version tags in headings say when each part was built (the full old file is `docs/archive/CLAUDE-before-split.md`, and version-by-version history is in REQUESTS.md). "See X" in the text names a section heading; the index in CLAUDE.md says which file holds each one. See also: `docs/timing-engine.md` (the engine), `docs/roster.md` (Bench, picker bar), `docs/training-and-paces.md` (workouts, Start workout).

## Stopwatch rows with one action button (3.17)
Replaces the 3.15 whole-row tap (the 3.15 section below is history where it differs: no press and hold, no trailing Undo zone, no two columns). All in app.js "watch cards", "actions" and "+ New, ⋯ menu". Nothing saved changed shape (new per-phone settings: `S.settings.compactRows`, `S.settings.lastPick`; `S.settings.compact`, the 3.6–3.16 two-column switch, stays in the data, unused).
- **Cause of the clipped time (3.15/3.16):** a row had a fixed height in rem with padding and gaps in px; its lines sat in a flex column and the time's line hid its overflow, so whenever the lines were taller than the row (two columns at the smaller iOS text sizes, a name wrapping to two lines) flexbox shrank that line and cut off the bottom of the digits (2.2 px at 14 px text in two columns). Since 3.17 no line can shrink: each line has a fixed height of its own, the row's height comes from its lines (never the other way round) and nothing around the time clips. `tests/e2e46.js` checks glyph ink against every clipping box.
- **A row** (`rowHTML()`, built once; `paintRow()` repaints in place; one column, full width, at least 72 pt): `.swipe` article > `.sw-trail` (Stop when running, Remove) + `.sw-front` = `.w-sel` (Edit mode) | `.w-bar` (team colour: `data-g` G, B, GB for a mixed group, from the runners' Girls/Boys; `genderKey()`) | `.w-info` (`.w-main`, an invisible button over the whole area, `data-act=menu`: opens the row's sheet; `.w-l1` name + `.w-cnt` "· 3"; `.w-l2` the large time `.w-big` + the rep count `.w-rp` ("Rep 2 of 4"); `.w-l3` = `.w-ul` the Undo link + `.w-st` the status line) | `.w-act` the action button (a third of the row, full height: `.w-al` Start / Lap / Go now / Done / Stopped, `.w-as` "at 400m", "rest 0:45", "lap 4"; `data-act` start / split / gonow / done / stopped; Done and Stopped look off and record nothing). Row `data-k` (idle, run, due, late, rest, paused, done) colours the status line.
- **Status line** (one line, the newest note else the state's own words, longest wording first): running with a plan "400m target 1:12 · due in 0:08" / "+3.9 past target"; after a lap for 6 s the lap with its signed difference (`lapWords()`: "Rep 2 · 400m · 1:12.4 · −0.6 vs target", without a plan "Lap 3 · 1:12.4 · +1.2 vs lap 2"); resting "Resting 0:45 · rep 3 next"; "Double tap ignored"; another coach's lap with their name, which outranks the lap's difference ("0:12.4 · Coach Cal", then "· Cal"). A waiting row: "Waiting · 4 × 800m · target 2:40 · 400m at 1:20" (or "Paces differ by 3.4%" first for a group). The rep count is on the time's line (no room for it in the status line at 390 pt); in Compact rows it leads the status line instead.
- **Text always fits** (`fitText(node, key, el, cands, avail)`): the longest wording that fits, else the longest that fits at ≥ 80% size, else the shortest as small as it takes (measured again at each size: the system font is relatively wider when small). Widths and fonts are read once per size change (`rowGeo()`, a ResizeObserver on each row's lines and on `#remProbe`, a 10rem probe for the iOS text size), words are measured on a canvas (`measW()`), so a frame never reads layout. Names: a runner "Maya Lopez" → "Maya L." → "Maya" (`nameCands()`); other names lose words from the end ("Waiting group…"). Lines use `white-space:nowrap` with `overflow:hidden` only as a backstop; no `text-overflow:ellipsis` anywhere in a row.
- **Taps:** only `.w-act` records. Touch-down time (`touchTime(e)`), commit on lift under `TAP_SLOP` (10 px), the action shown at touch-down, a second touch on the same button within `DOUBLE_MS` (1.5 s) ignored ("Double tap ignored"); a mouse click always counts; a click with no touch (keyboard, VoiceOver, `el.click()` in tests) acts at click time (`rowTap(w, at, act, key)`). The rest of the row: a click opens `rowSheet(w)` (a scroll never makes a click); a swipe left uses the generic `.swipe` handler (3.0) and shows `[data-sw=stop]` / `[data-sw=del]`; a tap on a swiped row closes it. No press and hold.
- **Undo:** after this phone's lap, the `.w-ul` link at the left end of the status line for 6 s (`LAPUNDO`; a 44 pt touch area inside the row via `::before`); also for 8 s after Start over, Stop (`stopRow()`: Undo takes the stop back as if it never happened: local `startAt` restored, shared the stop tap gets a removal version), a split or a rejoin (`TUNDO`, `tileUndo()`). Always in the row's sheet ("Undo lap 3 (2:24.1)", off with a reason when there's nothing). Remove (sheet or swipe) leaves "Removed … · Undo" in the row's place at its height (`ACT.del` fixes the placeholder's height).
- **Row sheet** (`rowSheet(w)`; `cardMenu()` and `lapSheet()` call it): Undo, Stop / Keep timing / Start over, Assign a runner… (a stopwatch with no runners, any state: `openBench({watch})` renames "Stopwatch n" / "Runner n" to the runner) or Change runner… (waiting), Change workout, Targets…, Send runners now…, Split runners…, Rejoin group, Share with coaches, Rename, Remove stopwatch (also while running), then every lap or split (`splitTable` / `lapTable`, a second coach's merged tap can be chosen). Item keys stay `data-m` (stop, resume, reset, undo, members, plan, targets, send, splitr, rejoin, share, rename, del).
- **+ = the runner picker** (`rosterPicker()`; the empty tab's "Choose runners" `[data-pick]`): search (`#rpQ`), "Same runners as last time (n)" (`S.settings.lastPick = {ids, mode, wk}`, only free runners), Girls / Boys / No Girls/Boys yet sections with Select all / Deselect all (`.rp-all`), iOS list rows with a check circle (`.rp-row[aria-pressed]`; busy runners dimmed "on Group 1"), a sticky footer: One per runner / One group (`[data-mode]`), Workout: None ⌄ (`#rpWk`, the 3.16 pull-down), Share with coaches, Start N stopwatches / Add without starting. One per runner names each after its runner; One group is "Group n" (`nextGroupName()`). Below: Quick stopwatch (no runner) "Stopwatch n" (was "Runner n"), Workout with pace groups… (the workout flow), Race… (`[data-new]` as before).
- **Header** (`updateToolbar()`): "Workout name · 9 stopwatches" (or "· mixed workouts"), then small buttons on one line: End workout, Show: All, and one bulk button on the right: Start all n waiting, else Stop all n (never both). Labels shorten at large text ("Start 3", "End", "Show"); only if even those don't fit does the row wrap.
- **Compact rows** (Settings > Display and sound, `S.settings.compactRows`, `body.rows-compact`): name and time on one line, the status line under them (about 56 pt). Replaces "Two columns".
- Test hooks: `MSApp.rowState(id)` = `{act, label, sub, k, name (as shown), big, rep, st, undo, bar}`; `watchMeta()` adds `status`, `elapsed`, `sh`; `tapRow(id)` taps the action button (keyboard-style). Tests: `tests/e2e46.js` (text fit in every state at 14–28 px, WebKit and Chrome; the button, Undo, sheet, swipe; the picker), `tests/e2e44.js` (real touch: tap storm with remote taps, scrolling, swipe, double taps, Undo link, layout). `tests/tools/tilestates.js` builds every state (now with due, late, a long name and a long mark); e2e34 (3.6.1 tiles) is retired. Screenshots `docs/screenshots-stopwatch-v2/` (`node tests/tools/shots317.js <before|after> [url]`).

## Private stopwatches and the Shared workouts section (3.16; the rest of 3.16 is in docs/training-and-paces.md)
All in app.js "3.16: the paces screen's pull-down menus, and a workout started from it" (before the Targets sheet) and "shared stopwatches (3.3)". No rules change; nothing saved changed shape (new: `w.share`, `S.settings.shareWatches`, `S.settings.wkMine`; older versions ignore them).
- **Private unless shared:** `newWatch(name, wk, share)` sets `w.share` from `share` or `S.settings.shareWatches` (default off) on a team; `shEnsure()` only shares a stopwatch with `w.share`, `shPush()` only those. The "Share with coaches" switch (`shareRowHTML()` / `bindShareRow()`) is on the + New sheet, the empty Stopwatches tab, the workout flow's step 2 and the Start sheet, and sets the remembered setting; ⋯ menu "Share with coaches" shares one stopwatch later (its taps so far go along). A stopwatch already shared (`w.sh`, also every one shared by 3.3–3.15) stays shared until ended; turning the setting off changes nothing for it. Split-offs inherit their group's sharing.
- **Shared workouts section:** shared rows get `.is-sh` (CSS `order:-1`) under the heading "Shared workouts" with "On this phone" above the rest (`gridSecs()`, only on a team with shared rows; called from `updateToolbar()` and `collapseRow()`). Another coach's new shared stopwatch arrives with `_remoteNew` → `w._pend`, hidden (`.pending`) until nobody has touched the screen for `IDLE_MS` (`revealWhenIdle()`), then slides in above, so no row moves under a finger.

## Shared stopwatches (3.3)
On a team every stopwatch (individual or group) is shared live with every coach phone (3.3–3.15; since 3.16 only stopwatches shared with "Share with coaches", see Paces menus, Start workout, private stopwatches (3.16)). All in app.js "shared stopwatches (3.3)" and sync.js "shared stopwatches (3.3)".
- **Data:** a header `teams/{t}/watches/{id}` = `{name, workoutId, athleteIds, athleteNames, autoName, day, ep}` (soft delete only) and one append-only document per tap `teams/{t}/wevents/{id}` = `{w, day, ep, type 'start'|'lap'|'gonow'|'stop'|'resume', at, off, by (uid), byName, plan (start only, JSON text)}`; a tap is changed only by a new version (`deleted`/`chosen` + `hist` via arrayUnion) or by its own phone filling in `off` once. On the phone: `w.sh`, `w.ev` (events), `w.ep` (epoch: Start over = +1, its Undo goes back), `w.day`, `w.hs` (the header as last sent/received, so only changes are written; `shPush()` after every save).
- **Replay:** `replayWatch(w)` rebuilds status, startAt, pausedT, run and plan from the current epoch's events, sorted by server time (`at + off`), converted to this phone's time (minus this phone's `CLOCK.off`), through `ACT` on a virtual clock: a start sets the clock (a second start is ignored) with the plan copy from the start event, a rest that ended before the next tap starts the next rep at its end (as `tick()` does), stop/resume/lap/gonow go through `ACT`. So every phone shows the same time and laps, a phone with a wrong clock included, and a reload or an update rebuilds everything from the saved events. Laps carry `tap` (`byName`, `alts`), no-plan laps `run.lapTaps[]`.
- **Taps:** `runAct()` → `shAct()` for shared stopwatches (start, split, gonow, stop, resume, undo = the last counted tap and any merged with it get a removal version; reset → `shReset()`); `startWatch(w, at)` for Start all / the workout flow / Quick stopwatch; Stop all, Clear all times, Clear finished, Remove and their Undos go through the header. Two coaches' Lap taps within `SH_MERGE` (2 s): one lap, the earlier counts unless one is `chosen` (`lapClusters()`); the card's lap list shows the other tap ("also Coach Cal (+0.4 s) · use this", `shChoose()`).
- **Sharing starts by itself:** `shEnsure(w)` (on any action, and from `shPush()`) gives a local stopwatch a header and turns its times so far into events. `shPush()` shares at once only stopwatches made on the team (`w.tm`, set by `newWatch()`) or with times; an idle one from before joining stays local until it's used. Remote headers and events arrive through `MSApp.watchRemote()` (`shRemoteHeader()`, `shRemoteEvent()`; events seen for a stopwatch are kept in `SH_SEEN` so a restored stopwatch gets its taps back). Listeners cover `day >= today − 2`.
- **Clocks:** sync.js measures the offset when team sync starts; events saved before it get it (`shClockFixed()`). **Offline:** taps queue in Firestore's offline cache and replay locally at once. "Coach X offline" (`coachNote()`): a coach who tapped today whose `devices/{uid}.seen` is over 150 s old; phones with a running shared stopwatch write `seen` once a minute (`SYNC.heartbeat()`), the only write not caused by a tap.
- **Show** (`showSheet()`, `S.settings.watchShow`): All, or chosen groups (by name), runners and other stopwatches; this phone only.
- **Rules** (3.3.0): `watches` and `wevents` (members; headers soft delete only; taps append-only). Before they're published every write is refused once (`watchBlocked`), quietly, and each phone keeps timing alone; a phone needs to reopen the app after publishing.
- Tests: `tests/e2e28.js` (three phones, one 37 s off), rules.test.mjs.

## Splitting runners out of a group (3.6; the 3.6 fixed tiles are history, in REQUESTS.md under 3.6.0)
- **Splitting runners out of a group** (app.js "splitting runners out of a group (3.6)"): `splitOut(g, ids, {now, at})` makes a new stopwatch right after the group (and its other split-offs) with `from = g.id`, its runners, the group's counted taps copied (`countedEvents()`: shared = the current epoch with merged laps as one; local = rebuilt from the times as `shEnsure` does) and replayed (`replayWatch`) with the runners' own plan (`planForIds()`: the workout compiled for them when its marks and reps match the group's plan, else the group's plan); `now` adds a `gonow` at the tap (sent during rest). The group's runners are updated (`setMembers()`: names, and its name if it was the automatic one). Membership log `w.memLog` = `[{t (elapsed ms), leave|join: ids}]`: local stopwatches keep it directly, shared ones derive it in `replayWatch` from `leave`/`join` taps (with `ids`). `membersAt(w,t)` = who was on it at a moment.
  - Name tap during rest: `sendNow()`; a second name within 2 s (`SPLIT_MS` = `SH_MERGE`) undoes the first split quietly and splits both together at the first tap's time (`LASTSEND`); if that would take everyone, the group starts its next rep at that time instead (`gonowAt()`).
  - ⋯ > Split runners… (`splitSheet()`, running or stopped groups of 2+): any time, one stopwatch each or together, keeping the group's place (no early start). ⋯ > Rejoin group (`rejoin()`, a split-off stopwatch whose group is on the track, `groupOf()`): the split-off stops at that moment (shared: a stop tap), a `leave` on it and a `join` on the group, `hid = true` (hidden everywhere, kept for practice history, `nameOf` keeps the names, `athleteIds` = [] so `heldBy()` frees the runners); Clear finished and End workout remove it.
  - Undo: every split and rejoin has a one-tap Undo on the tiles (`tileUndo`, 8 s): the split-off's header soft-deleted and the group's `leave` removed as a version (local: the memLog entry), or the rejoin's taps removed as versions and `hid` cleared. Local and shared take the same steps.
  - Other phones: the header with `from` is inserted after its group (`shRemoteHeader`), a `hid` change rebuilds the grid; both with the slide and the tap guard. Before the 3.6 rules are published, split writes are refused quietly (`SYNC.splitsBlocked()`, separate from `watchBlocked`, so sharing everything else goes on) and splitting says the rules are needed.
  - **Practice history:** `historyRecord()` adds `ids` per stopwatch and, when its runners changed, `who: {sets, s (per split), l (per lap), names}` plus `from`/`rejoined`. `practiceRows(h, id)` = one runner's splits and laps across every stopwatch they were on (copies made at a split have no runners, so nothing counts twice); practice history entries have "By runner" (`byRunnerHTML()`), and the runner card in Data has "Practice" (`runnerPracticeHTML()`, the latest 8 sessions).
- Tests: `tests/e2e32.js` (layout), `tests/e2e33.js` (splits, three phones, and a phone without a team), `tests/tools/phones.js` (team phones for emulator suites), rules.test.mjs (351).

## Card lap/split list
- **Since 3.6 the tile has no expanding list:** its lap area shows the latest one or two laps or splits (`lapAreaHTML()`), and a tap opens all of them in a sheet (`lapSheet()`, newest first with liveLog; a second coach's merged tap can be chosen there). With liveLog off the area shows only the count. The rest of this section is the 1.1–3.5 behaviour, kept for the sheet's tables.
- `S.settings.liveLog` ("Show splits as you go", default on; `load()` fills it in for old saves). When on, the list opens at the first lap, shows newest first, and stays open unless collapsed (`SHUT[id]`, cleared when the list empties). When off, it stays collapsed unless opened (`OPEN[id]`).
- `splitTable`/`lapTable(…, newest)` are shared with the Results tab (which stays oldest first). Columns with class `c-x` (Target, Section, Total) are hidden on cards in compact view.

Goal: a coach who has never seen the app can use it. The timing engine is unchanged (only label strings inside `updateLive()` changed).

A small ↶ undo stays next to it. The runners line shows only when it adds something (a group, or a renamed card).

## Stopwatch rows: one tap always does the right thing (3.15; superseded by 3.17 where they differ)
Replaces the 2.13–3.14 tiles (sections below that describe tile buttons, the lap area, `ARM`, `TILE_GUARD`, `PRESS`/`PEND` and FLIP guards are history). All in app.js "watch cards" and "actions".
- **A row per stopwatch, built once** (`rowHTML()`): `.w-tap` (a button covering the whole row; its `data-act` is the state's action: start / split / gonow / details), the trailing `.w-undo` zone, a visually hidden `.w-more` (VoiceOver / keyboard menu). `renderCard()` creates a row only once (always appended at the end) and otherwise calls `paintRow()`, which writes text, attributes and classes in place (name + runner count, pill `data-k` idle/run/due/late/rest/paused/done with an icon and words, the large rep time, "Rep 2/4 · 200m · target 0:40" from `planOf()` = each runner's own target, the status line = the newest note (`tileMsg()`: lap notes, `rowMsg()`, split notes, Undo messages, newest by `at`) else what a tap does). No size in styles.css depends on the state. `updateLive()` (called by `tick()`) now just calls `paintRow()`; the engine math is unchanged.
- **Taps** (grid pointer handlers): the time is the touch-down's own timestamp (`touchTime(e)`); the tap commits on lift if the finger moved < `TAP_SLOP` (10 px), with the action the row showed at touch-down (`rowTap(w, at, act, undo)`). A local lap runs `ACT.split`/`ACT.gonow` with `VNOW = at` (the engine reads time through `nowMs()`); shared stopwatches pass `at` to `shAct`; Start uses `startWatch(w, at)`. A second counted touch on a row within `DOUBLE_MS` (1.5 s) is ignored ("Double tap ignored"); a mouse click (a computer, and the puppeteer suites' `page.click`) always counts. A scroll (browser pointercancel or movement) records nothing; press and hold (`HOLD_MS` 550) or a sideways swipe (`SWIPE_X` 24 px) opens `cardMenu()`, and the click the lift makes is swallowed (`swallowClick`, trusted clicks near the lift point only). A click with no touch (keyboard, VoiceOver, `el.click()` in tests) acts at click time. Touch-ups are heard page-wide (a lift over a sheet still ends the touch).
- **Undo zone**: live for 6 s after a lap (`LAPUNDO`) or 8 s after Start over / a split / a rejoin (`TUNDO`); otherwise it is part of the row's tap. `rowUndo()`.
- **Nothing moves under a finger**: no tap guards; a removed row keeps its place ("Removed … · Undo", or empty) and `collapseRow()` closes it only after `IDLE_MS` (1.5 s) with no touch; split-offs and other coaches' new stopwatches are appended at the end (`splitOut`, `shRemoteHeader`; 3.6–3.14 inserted them after their group); the bar above the rows has fixed lines (`.wb-wk` workout name, `.wb-btns` scrolling buttons with `visibility` instead of `display`, `.coach-note`); the race banner sits below the rows; the Update and Install banners stay hidden on the Stopwatches tab while it has stopwatches (`body.has-watches.on-watches`).
- **Menu** (`cardMenu()`): Stop (one tap), Keep timing, Start over, Undo, Change workout / runners / Targets, **Send runners now…** (`sendSheet()`: big name buttons, one tap each, the touch-down time goes to `sendNow(g, id, t0)`), Split runners…, Rejoin group, **Lap list**, Rename, Remove. A finished or stopped row's tap opens `lapSheet()` with Keep timing / Start over.
- **Settings**: Display and sound > "Two columns" (`S.settings.compact`, the same whole-tile tap; 3.17: "Compact rows" instead) and "Click on each tap" (`S.settings.tapClick`, `tapClick()`).
- **Sizes**: `--row-h` / `--u-w` live on `#grid` (rows, the Undo zone, and a removed row's placeholder all use them). Two columns: the tile is a size container (fonts `min(rem, cqi)`), the pill shows its short words (`.w-pts`: "due 0:08", "late +3.9", "rest 0:45"), and the live Undo zone overlays the right edge. `#grid` is a size container too: below 19rem across (large iOS text) a full-width row puts its pill under the name and grows to 8.4rem.
- Test hooks: `MSApp.rowAction(id, a)`, `sendRunner(id, aid)`, `tapRow(id)` (a keyboard-style tap), `myTaps(id)`, `lapCount(id)`, `rowState(id)`. Tests: `tests/e2e44.js` (tap storm, scrolling, double taps, layout, two coaches; real touch input), `tools/tilestates.js` builds every row state; screenshots `docs/screenshots-stopwatch/` (`node tests/tools/shots315.js <before|after> [url]`). The two-coach drill is in REQUESTS.md (3.15.0).
