# Season summaries and awards (3.18)

Data > Highlights > Season (celebrations, shareable) and Awards (coaches only, never shared). All in app.js "season summaries and awards (3.18)", right after the latest-meet section; sync.js `awardData()` and the `awards` kind; firestore.rules `awardOk()` and `match /awards/{docId}`. See also: `docs/data-and-charts.md` (Highlights, race-day ratings, `teamRows()`), `docs/import.md` (official results, `offLevel()`), `docs/meets-and-schedule.md` (the Schedule and the meet editor).

## Placement
- Data > Highlights has a segmented switch **Latest meet | Season** (3.19: **| Share**, docs/banquet-and-exports.md) (`hlModeHTML()`, `[data-hlm]`, `S.settings.hlMode`, per phone, remembered; Latest meet is the default, so the 3.8–3.13 page is unchanged). `renderLatest()` draws the switch and the chosen mode; the switch hides while a pushed page (a runner's latest-meet page `hlRunner`, a runner's season page `ssRunner`, or Awards `ssAwards`) is open. `navBar()` shows ‹ Highlights and the runner's name (or "Awards"); `hlBack()`, a tap on the Data tab and leaving Highlights close all three.
- The season is the one picked in the nav bar's season picker (`ssSeason()` = `tvSeason` or the current season); changing it redraws Season.

## Season (celebrations, shareable)
- `seasonRes(a, Y)`: one result per runner per race day of the season (high school, 3000 m and up, official preferred, from `resultsFor()`); `teamOrder(Y, g)`: team order on each race day within Girls / Boys by pace.
- `seasonStory(a, Y)` (memoized): at 5K when the runner raced one, else their most common distance:
  - **arc**: opener → season best (raw), and `arcAdj` course- and weather-adjusted (`adjPace()`, both race days rated);
  - **PRs** and **season bests** set during the season (a race faster than every earlier result at that distance, the 3.12 rule), **milestones** (first 5K under N:00, `MILESTONE_MIN`);
  - **best adjusted race** (lowest adjusted pace, 2+ rated races);
  - **season over season**: last season's best vs this one (`sos`), the same meets a year apart (by series), the career PR by season (`career`);
  - **championship meets** (`isChamp()`), **team place** on each race day, **consistency** (races within 3% of the season-best pace, adjusted when every race is rated);
  - badges `hl` (PR count, Sub-N, arc, vs last season, Championship, Consistent) and a one-sentence **story** built from the numbers with the runner's first name (never a pronoun).
- `ssTeam(Y, g)`: Girls and Boys cards: the mean top-5 average and 1–5 spread over the season's full meets (`teamRows()` raw), the best and tightest meet, the first-to-last change (adjusted when rated, else raw on one course), total PRs and season bests.
- Screen: team cards, then Girls / Boys sections of rows (alphabetical, so no implicit ranking): name, two badges, "−1:12.4 since the opener", the season best. A row opens `ssDetailHTML()` (story, comparison cells, the season arc list with SB / best race / championship badges and team place, highlights, best adjusted race, team place, season over season, Share, the profile link). The Awards entry (lock icon) is at the bottom.
- **Share** (`ssShare()`, Copy / Share…): `ssTeamText(Y, g)` for the team (the Share button at the top) and `ssRunnerText(st)` per runner (on the runner's page). **Celebrations only**: never a coach note, a health reason, an award, a recipient, a Captain, good standing or varsity status. No emoji. `tests/e2e47.js` checks every recap against a list of coach-only words.

## Awards (coaches only, lock icon, never shared)
`awardsHTML(Y)` from `awData(Y, team)` (memoized; team 'G', 'B', or 'A' when Girls and Boys are together). The page says "Coaches only · never shared" with a lock; nothing on it reaches any share text.
- **Varsity or JV per result (3.24: Varsity by default).** `rowLevel(M, row)` gives one result row's level and where it came from, `resLevel(a, x)` one runner's result for a race day (the official list wins in `resultsFor()`, the hand-timed race beside it as `x.hand`; a coach's version on either wins, the newest first). The order:
  1. a coach's **level version on that result**: a hand-timed race's `{op:'level', rid, level, uid, dev, byName, at}` edit (`modelOf()` → `M.levels`, `M.levelLog`; `''` clears) or an official record's `{op:'level', k, level}` edit (`levelEdits()`); append-only, with the coach's name and time, synced (`setRaceLevels()`, `setLevels()`; `applyLevelChanges()` batches both kinds with one Undo);
  2. a coach's **choice for that race day made in 3.18–3.23** (the awards `v-<date>` document), kept and still honoured (`lvLegacy()`); 3.24 writes no new ones;
  3. a **JV tag from race setup** (`row.jv`, saved with the race), a **JV race** (division `GJV` / `BJV`), or an **imported JV label** on the official result (`parseDiv()`: "Junior", "Junior V", "JV", frosh/soph, open, reserve…; a label printed for the other gender isn't trusted, 2.11.1);
  4. otherwise **Varsity**. Middle school results have no level and never count.
  Nothing waits to be confirmed: the awards calculate right away (`awData().unknown` is always empty; the spreadsheet's Awards tracker is never blank). **Review check:** a race day where a team has more than 7 runners counted as Varsity is flagged (`awData().flags`: date, team, meet, n) at the top of the Awards page with a one-tap **Review** that opens that meet's Edit Varsity / JV, in Data health (kind `jv`) and as a warning line in the spreadsheet.
  - **Edit Varsity / JV** (`jvEditSheet({Y, date, meetId, g})`, class `.jv-sheet`): one race day in one pass, Girls and Boys as separate sections (or one team from a Review flag), every runner's time with the Official badge and where the level came from, Varsity | JV per runner, All Varsity / All JV per team, "Save n changes" (off with a reason until something changes) → `applyLevelChanges()`: one batch, one Undo ("8 levels set · Undo"; Undo appends clearing versions, nothing is rewritten). Reached from Data › Meets (the Edit Varsity/JV button on every race and official list), the Awards page (**Varsity / JV by meet**, `awVarSheet()`: a row per race day and team with "n Varsity · n JV of n" and a flag over 7; Review), and Data health. The level is written on the hand-timed race when the team timed the runner that day (any member may append there), else on the official record (`levelTarget()`; rules 3.24.0 let any member append a level there too).
  - **A runner's details:** the time editor (`timeSheet`) of a saved high school race has a **Level** switch (Varsity | JV) at the finish cell, saved at once as a version with Undo.
  - **Display:** a small **JV** badge (`bdg('jv', 'JV')`, icon + word) only on that runner's result rows (the race results table and cards, Data › Meets, official lists, the runner card's season list) and in the Awards sheets. Never on stopwatches, the race grid, charts, Highlights, shared recaps or graphics (e2e53 checks).
  - **Race setup** (3.24, docs/race-mode.md): press and hold a picked name → Mark as JV / Mark as Varsity; **Mark JV…** (a switch per picked runner, Girls / Boys); **This is the JV race** (tags everyone picked; a runner added to an all-JV race is JV too). Tags live on `S.race.runners[].jv` (sync.js `raceData` passes it; the race document's runner items aren't validated by the rules, so no rules change), `raceModel()` copies them to the saved rows.
- **Season's meets** (`total`, Awards settings): "Meets with a varsity race" (default: race days where this team had a runner counted as Varsity) or "Varsity meets on the schedule" (scheduled with a Varsity level, attending, up to today).
- **Most Valuable Runner** (`mvrCalc(rows, total, thr)`, pure, `MSApp.mvrCalc`): each runner's place among our varsity runners in each varsity race (by pace, within Girls / Boys), the average, eligible at `thr`% (default 80) of `total`; ranked lowest average first, the math shown ("2,1,3,1,2,2,2,3 = 16 (2.0)"; `mvrAvgTxt()`), equal averages flagged "Tie: coaches decide", runners under the threshold listed apart, and a note pointing at the Review flags when a race has more than 7 runners counted as Varsity (3.24).
- **Varsity Letter** (checklist per runner): varsity races vs the season's meets (majority?), varsity at a Championship meet (Conference, Sectionals, State), senior (grade 12) with 3–4 high school seasons, good standing confirmed yes / no. Tapping a runner opens `awRunnerSheet()`: Captain this season, good standing Yes / No / Not set (only the yes or no is stored: no notes, nothing academic), grade and high school seasons (steppers; "Use the history" clears a coach's value).
  - Grade: a coach's value, else `gradeNow()` from the official results moved back to that season. Seasons out (`hsSeasons()`): a coach's value, else the number of seasons with high school results up to this one (this one counted).
- **Most Improved Runner** (3.24: **one list per team**, since one award is given a year for Girls and one for Boys: each criterion gives rank points to its top five (1st = 5 … 5th = 1), the points are summed (`awData().mir`: `list` with `pts`, `rank`, `parts`; `mirMath()` shows the sum, `mirSub()` the figures), the highest total carries a Recommended badge, equal totals are a tie for the coaches; the Mark buttons and recipients (`recip['G.mir']`) are unchanged, so earlier selections stay). The evidence, top five each: season-long (opener → season best, adjusted; raw only when every race was on one course), season over season (last season's best → this season's), biggest PR drops (best before Aug 1 → this season's best). Note: "Practice approach and academics are the coaches' call." Nothing academic is stored.
- **Rookie of the Year**: runners in their first high school season (`hsSeasons() === 1`, from the history or set to 1), with varsity races, team places and opener-to-best improvement. Note: "Practice participation and enthusiasm are the coaches' call."
- **Mustang Award**: coaches' choice; lists every runner who isn't a Captain or marked for MVR or Most Improved, with their season best and highlights, so no one is overlooked.
- **Recipients**: a Mark button on each candidate (`awToggle()`, Undo bar); marked names show under each award's heading and in the worksheet. **3.25:** they are the only names the Season Awards graphics and the book's Awards page use (`awShow(Y, team)`); Captains (`awRun().captain`) get their own slide.
- **Share awards and the announcement hold (3.25):** the Awards page has **Share awards** (`[data-awshare]` → the share studio on the Season Awards design at 4:5) and an **Awards announced** switch (`[data-awann]`, admins and phones without a team; `awAnnounce(Y, on)` writes `announced`, `announcedAt`, `announcedBy` on the season's `s-<Y>` document, synced; an Undo in the bar; `awAnnounced(Y)`). Until it is on, the studio previews every slide but Share this slide and Share all are off with "Awards haven’t been announced yet." (and a lock line under the toolbar). Recipients and captains are never changed by announcing.
- **Evidence slides (3.26, `[data-awev]` → `awEvidenceShare(Y, k)`):** every award section has an **Evidence slide** button under the recipient line: a 16:9 PNG (1920 × 1080, `gfxCanvas({design:'evidence', size:'wide', subj:k})`) that states the award's criteria in the coach's wording (`AW_CRIT`) and shows the marked recipients' figures (`awEvidence(Y, k, team)`: per team with a recipient, never anyone else, never a ranking): **MVR** the places with the total and the average exactly as the page ("2,1,3,1,2,2,2,3 = 16 (2.0)", `mvrCalc().math`) and each race as "nth of n"; **Most Improved** opener → season best with the adjusted drop, last season's best → this one, the PR drop, a progression chart (and the career chart when there are earlier seasons; `bkChart()`); **Rookie** first high school season, varsity races of the meets, team places, the improvement, the chart; **Varsity Letter** a table (runner, varsity races of the meets with "majority", championship meets, seasons, grade; one font size for the whole table); **Mustang Award** the coach's **Banquet citation** (a 400-character field per team under the Mustang section, `[data-awcite]` → `awCiteSet()`; kept on the season document as `cite`, synced; the button says "Write a citation below first" until one exists). The button is off with "Mark a recipient first" until a recipient is marked. Not held by Awards announced (it is an export for the banquet screen, like the worksheet), not in the studio's gallery, no caption. The same drawing (`awEvBody()`) makes the slideshow book's evidence pages (docs/banquet-and-exports.md). Palette and background follow the studio's style.
- **Icons (3.25, `AW_ICON`, `awIc()`):** Most Valuable Runner a trophy, Most Improved a trending-up arrow, Rookie of the Year a star, Varsity Letter a letter badge (`letter-badge`, drawn for the app), Mustang Award the school's Mustang mark (`brand/`, as a CSS mask in HTML and the SVG on a canvas), Captain a "C" armband (`armband`); the same set in the Awards section headings, the captain badge, the graphics and the book.
- **Awards settings** (`awSetSheet()`, the same for every coach): Girls and Boys separately (default on), the MVR threshold (50–100%, steps of 5, default 80), what counts as the season's meets.
- **Awards worksheet** (`awWorksheetCanvases()` → `pdfPages()`, `awExport()` → `shareFile()`): letter-size pages, one section per award per team with the math, the checklists, the evidence, the notes and "Recipient: ____" lines (or the marked names). The footer of every page says "Coaches only, never shared".

## Championship meets
- `isChamp(meet)`: a coach's choice (`m-<meet id>` doc), else the default: the NEC Conference, WIAA Sectional and WIAA State series (`CHAMP_SERIES`) or a meet kind naming Conference / Sectional / State meet.
- The meet editor (Schedule) has a "Championship meet" switch; untouched, it follows the series (choosing a series updates it), and only a touched switch is saved. Schedule rows show a Championship badge (trophy).
- Kept with the awards rather than on the meet document, so the meets rules (and older phones) don't change.

## Data (synced kind `awards`, `S.awards`)
One small document each, so two coaches rarely change the same one (last write wins per document; an arriving document replaces the local one whole, so a field unset on another phone is unset here):
- `s-<season>` `{k:'s', season, set:{split, thr, total}, recip:{'G.mvr':[ids], 'B.letter':[…], 'A.mustang':[…]}, announced?, announcedAt?, announcedBy?, cite?:{'G.mustang': text, 'B.mustang': text}}` (3.25: the announcement hold; 3.26: the Mustang Award citations, the one free-text field in awards, written to be read aloud)
- `r-<season>-<runner>` `{k:'r', season, aid, captain?, standing?:'yes'|'no', hs?, grade?}`
- `v-<date>` `{k:'v', season, date, var:{runner: 'V'|'JV'}}` (written by 3.18–3.23; 3.24 reads it as a fallback and writes levels on the results instead)
- `m-<meet id>` `{k:'m', season, meetId, champ}`

`awPut(id, base, patch)` creates or changes one (undefined removes a field) and saves. Never deleted (unmarking is an edit; sync.js `noDelete`). `migrate()` adds `awards: []`; older versions ignore it. Backups carry it with the rest of `S`.

**Rules (3.18.0):** `teams/{t}/awards/{id}`: any member reads and writes; only the fields above with their types (`awardOk()`); no deletes. **3.25.0** adds `announced` (bool), `announcedAt` (number) and `announcedBy` (≤ 30 chars); publish any time (fields only). **3.26.0** adds `cite` (a map with only `G.mustang` / `B.mustang` / `A.mustang`, each ≤ 400 chars); publish any time (a field only); until it is published a citation stays on the phone that wrote it (Sync log: refused) and the other phones' evidence slide and book show none. Before the 3.18 rules are published, the awards listener is refused and the phone keeps its awards decisions to itself, quietly (`quietKinds`, like places and weather in 3.2); after publishing, reopening the app sends them.
