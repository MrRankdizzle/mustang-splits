Race weather from Open-Meteo (allowed by rule 12), race locations, flags, and weather in race-day ratings.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-29 with its wording unchanged; the version tags in headings say when each part was built (the full old file is `docs/archive/CLAUDE-before-split.md`, and version-by-version history is in REQUESTS.md). "See X" in the text names a section heading; the index in CLAUDE.md says which file holds each one. See also: `docs/data-and-charts.md` (Race-day difficulty (3.1)).

## Race weather (3.2)
Weather for every race from **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com): free for non-commercial use, no API key, CORS. Data is CC BY 4.0, so every place weather appears shows `WX_ATTR` ("Weather data by Open-Meteo.com", linked). All in app.js "weather (3.2)". Nothing is on the timing path: the gun, taps and saves never wait for it.
- **Places** (`S.places`, synced kind `places`): one per course (id = course id) or per meet name without a course (middle school: `'n-'+slug(town words)`, time trials `n-time-trial`). `racePlaceId(x)`: the meet's course, the race's course, the series the importer knows from the name (`seriesOfName()` → `seriesCourse()`), else the town in the name (`townWords()`). Only **confirmed** places are used; a place confirmed without coordinates = skip (no weather). Settings > Weather > Race locations (`placesSheet()`, also Data health "Race locations"): every place once, with suggestions from Open-Meteo geocoding (`suggestPlace()`: `PLACE_HINT` by course name, e.g. Winagamie → Neenah, then the name's parts; Wisconsin, exact name; meet names are tried whole), a Map link, Confirm / Confirm all / type a town (Find) / I'm here (phone location) / Skip. `placeSet()`: a moved place refetches its weather.
- **Weather records** (`S.weather`, synced kind `weather`; a phone's `tries`/`next` stay local): id = `date_placeId`, or `date_g-<raceId>` for a race whose phone captured its location. `wxDays()` = every race day (HS and MS, `allRaces(true)`) with its window: start = the gun from a hand-timed race's taps (`gunOf()`), else the meet's time, else typical (weekdays 4:30 PM, weekends 9 AM); end = slowest finish (+30 min when several races ran after a scheduled start). `wxPlan()` creates waiting records for confirmed places (the backfill), `wxRun(why)` fills the ones whose race is over: grouped by place and season, the forecast API for the last 85 days, the archive before that (hourly, °F/mph/inch, UTC dates), `wxSummary()` = averages over the race (5-minute interpolation), highest gust, rain during and in the 48 h before (Open-Meteo precipitation = the hour before). A failed or offline fetch leaves it waiting with backoff (`next`); a server that doesn't answer is skipped for the rest of that run; `online` retries at once. Runs on open, return, every 5 min, `online`, and after End race. Filled records never change, so they work offline.
- **New races:** `wxGun(r)` at the gun (after it, never awaited): a record at the gun time with the course's place, or with Race setup's "Weather: use this phone's location at the gun" (`S.settings.raceGeo`, permission asked when switched on) the phone's position when it arrives. `wxEnded(r)` sets the end at End race; Undo gun removes it.
- **Flags** (`wxFlagsOf()`, thresholds `wxT()` = `S.settings.wx` over `WX_DEF`, Settings > Weather steppers): Warm temperature + dew point ≥ 130, Hot ≥ 150 (instead of Warm), Cold ≤ 35°F, Windy sustained ≥ 15 mph, Likely muddy ≥ 0.5″ in the 48 h before or any rain during. Icon + word chips (`flagChip()`); tapping one dismisses it for that race day (`wxDismiss()`, stored in the record's `dismissed`, synced, Undo; "n dismissed · show"). Context only: separate from coach tags and never leave a result out of anything.
- **Where:** race blocks (`wxBlock()`, via `wxHere()` in `rdLine()`: each meet, unlinked team races, Races on this phone, middle school lists), meet headers, "Weather at this meet, year by year" (`wxYearsHTML()`), runner card races, the runner season chart and the Team top-5 chart (`wxBand()`: an icon band above the plot, a key entry per flag with its rule), the Team meet table and Race-day ratings.
- **Race-day difficulty with weather** (`dayRatings0()`): a day's difficulty = weather `w` (Mark Hadley's temperature + dew point guide `HEAT_PTS`, coefficient 1; wind over 10 mph, rain before/during and cold under 40°F with costs fitted to the team's days, ridge toward `WX_PRIOR` worth `WX_PRIOR_DAYS`) + course and everything else `c` (centred, season-long drift removed, as in 3.1). A day without weather gets the season's average weather. Ratings are shown against the season's average race day; `wxPart[k]` = the weather's part (vs average weather), `wxSplitText()` "Weather explains 0:43 of it; course and everything else −0:07", `wxShare` = how much of the ratings' spread the weather explains.
- Tests: `tests/e2e27.js` (a fake Open-Meteo in the browser), rules.test.mjs (places, weather).
