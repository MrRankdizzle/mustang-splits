# Training and paces

The Training tab (ids `workouts`): pace math, team training paces, the pull-down menus, Start workout, today's goal and templates, effort parts, pace groups.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-29 with its wording unchanged; the version tags in headings say when each part was built (the full old file is `docs/archive/CLAUDE-before-split.md`, and version-by-version history is in REQUESTS.md). "See X" in the text names a section heading; the index in CLAUDE.md says which file holds each one. See also: `docs/timing-engine.md` (effort segments in the engine), `docs/training-rules.md` (the Start workout rules, checked by e2e45), `docs/stopwatches.md`.

## Paces menus and Start workout (3.16; private stopwatches are in docs/stopwatches.md)
All in app.js "3.16: the paces screen's pull-down menus, and a workout started from it" (before the Targets sheet) and "shared stopwatches (3.3)". No rules change; nothing saved changed shape (new: `w.share`, `S.settings.shareWatches`, `S.settings.wkMine`; older versions ignore them).
- **Menus** (`pulldown(btn, sections, cur, onPick)` / `closePulldown()`, `PD` is a `var` so `modal()` and `showTab()` can close it at any time): an iOS pull-down (`.pd-menu`, `role=menu`, `menuitemradio` with `data-pdk`, section headers `.pd-h`, ✓ on the current one, 44 pt items) under `#tpDBtn` "Distance: 800 m ⌄" and `#tpEBtn` "Effort: CV ⌄" (`.tp-pick`, side by side, stacked when the text is too large). Closes on an outside touch, Escape, scroll, a sheet or a tab change. `TP_DMENU` (Short, Middle, Long, Time, then Custom… → `tpCustomSheet()`), `TP_EMENU` (Aerobic, Steady, Fast, Race paces; slowest to fastest), `TP_ESHORT` button labels. The 3.14 chip rows are gone; `tpSel()` is unchanged. No sideways scroll on the Training tab at 320 pt or large text (3.16 also fixed `.goal-grid`, the today block and the tab labels, `min(.6875rem,15px)`).
- **Start workout** (`#tpStart` under the title, off for a time selection with `#tpStartWhy`): `tpStartSheet()` = the suggestion (`trainSuggest(sel)` → `trainRule(d, e, rt)` from `TRAIN`, rt = the rep time of the list's middle runner `teamMid()`), its reason, Reps stepper, Rest as Time (`#wsRest`) or Jog (`#wsJog`, meters; `jogSecs()` = the middle runner's easy pace, so the rest ends by itself), the total, the Share switch, Choose runners. The rules and sources are in `docs/training-rules.md` (its worked-examples table is checked by e2e45; change both together). The volume limit wins over everything (one rep when a single rep is over it); R and I rest stay within 1:00–5:00; Easy / Long run and Threshold of 2 miles or more are one continuous run.
- **Remembered numbers:** `S.settings.wkMine[tpKey(sel)] = {reps, rest, kind, jog}` (per distance and effort, this phone) only when they differ from the suggestion; the sheet says so and has "Back to the suggestion".
- **The workout** (`tpWorkout()`): an ordinary effort workout (`TP_REF` paceRef, `TP_EFF` effort word, check-ins (`cp`) every 400 m from 1600 m and every mile from 3000 m, `restJog` on the segment for a jog rest), reused when an identical one exists (so starts don't pile up workouts). `refPace()` knows `goal` (`goal5k()`; needs `prof.aid`, set by `paceProfile()`) and `2mi`. Then `openWorkoutFlow({workoutId, fixed:true, autoGroups:true})`: step 2 shows the workout name instead of the list ("Groups and start") and pre-suggests pace groups within Girls / Boys (the list's groups); every stopwatch gets its runner's (or group middle's) target from the engine as before.
- Test hooks: `MSApp.trainRule(d, e, rt)` (d meters, or 'mile' / '2mi'), `trainSuggest(sel)`, `addWatch(name, wk, share)`, `addGroup(name, wk, ids, share)`; `watchMeta().plan.rest`. `tests/lib.js tpPick(p, d, e)` picks through the menus. Tests: `tests/e2e45.js` (menus at 320/390 pt and large text, the doc's examples, remembered numbers, targets = engine, two phones: private never sent, shared within 2 s and laps from both); `tests/tools/phones.js` turns sharing on for the older team suites. ## Team training paces (3.14; the chip rows became menus in 3.16, see above)
All in app.js "Team training paces (3.14…)" (after `paceTableHTML()`). Nothing saved changed shape: new per-phone settings only.
- **Selection** (`tpSel()`, `S.settings.tpSel = {d, e, c}`, per phone, remembered; the 3.10 `S.settings.paceUnit` stays unused): `TP_D` distances 100 … 2 miles and times 1/2/3/5 min (`t60`…), plus `custom` (`c = {v, u}`, u m|mi|min, `tpCustomSheet()`: a stepper, `TP_CU`); `TP_E` efforts easy (range), long (the middle of Easy, `paces.easy`, Daniels runs long runs at E pace), threshold, cv, interval, repetition, 5k, 2mi (new in `pacesFor()`: `raceTimeAt(V, 2 mi)/2`), mile, goal. Two chip rows (`.tp-chips`, `[data-tpd]`, `[data-tpe]`, aria-pressed, 44 pt); a redraw keeps each row's scroll and always brings its selected chip into view. Title `tpTitle()` ("800 m at CV pace", "3 min at threshold pace").
- **Values** (`tpView(sel)`): per runner `tpSpm()` from `paceProfile()` (the same paces the profile's pace table and the workouts' `refPace()` use; `MILE` = 1609.34 everywhere). A distance → `tpT()` = `fmtSec(spm × m / MILE)`, tenths at 200 m and under (like `paceTableHTML()`), else whole seconds, always m:ss; Easy "fast–slow". A time → the distance covered (meters to 5 m, or miles with 2 decimals once anyone covers 3000 m). Sections Girls / Boys / not marked; fastest first; `groupByPace()` (the 2.9% rule, now shared with `suggestGroups()`), each group its own `.tp-list` card with its number on every row (`.tp-pg`); runners without a profile last, "Needs a race".
- **Goal 5K** (`goal5k(a, p)`): `S.settings.goal5k[id]` (seconds, this phone only; set or cleared in the runner sheet), else 1% faster than the predicted 5K.
- **Runner sheet** (`tpRunnerSheet(id)`): the basis + VDOT, this selection's value, the goal field (goal effort), the profile (`openProf('workouts', id)`), or for Needs a race the reason and Add a time trial.
- **Share** (`tpShare()`): this view as a PNG (`tpViewCanvas()`), or the full pace chart as a PDF (`tpChartCanvases()`: one page per effort, every runner at 200 … mile; `pdfPages()` = a multi-page PDF of JPEGs, `pdfOf()` kept as its one-page form).
- `MSApp.teamPaces(sel)`, `paceEngine(id)`, `workoutPace(id, ref)`, `paceGroups(ids, ref)`, `pacePdf()`, `paceImage()` for tests (`tests/e2e43.js`, e2e38). Screenshots: `docs/screenshots-paces/` (`node tests/tools/shots314.js <before|after> [url]`).

## Training tab (3.9)
- The Workouts tab is called **Training** on screen (tab bar, `NAV_TITLE`, Settings "Training and paces"); its ids stay `workouts` (`data-tab`, `#v-workouts`, `SET_PAGES` key).
- `GOALS` = six types: base, threshold, speed (VO2max), rep (speed/repetition), sharpen, recovery (recovery/pre-meet; `LEGACY_GOAL` maps the 2.14 goals long/premeet). `WK_TPL` has 5–7 sessions per type, all effort parts (`P_(dist, ref, cp, effort, pct)`; `pct` = steady % of 5K speed), each with `why` and `note`. Never remove or reuse an id (saved workouts carry `tpl`).
- `suggestionsFor()` returns every option (schedule-fit first); `todayHTML()` shows 3 until "Show all" (`todayAll`). `tplVolume()` = distance at the target paces ("4.8 km at pace"), shown on each option and in `tplSheet()` (follows the reps stepper).
- **Favorites:** `S.settings.favWk` (workout ids, this phone only; `isFav()`/`setFav()`), `#wkFavs` at the top of the tab (above today's goal), Favorite in the row's tap menu and trailing swipe, "☆ Save to favorites" in `tplSheet()` (saves through `wkFromTpl()`). One click handler `wkRowClick` serves `#wkList` and `#wkFavs`.
- `MSApp.tplIds()`, `tplVolumes()` for tests (`tests/e2e37.js`).

## Workouts that plan themselves (2.14)
- The Workouts tab (Training since 3.9) starts with "What's today's goal?" (`todayHTML()` in `#wkToday`, above the workout list): seven goals (`GOALS`), the schedule line (`schedule()`: the next meet, today included, and the last one before today, in days), the goal the schedule suggests (`suggestedGoal()`: race day / 1 day before → Pre-meet; 1 day after → Recovery; 2 days before → Race sharpening; 2 days after → Aerobic base; ≤4 days before → Threshold/CV; otherwise Speed), and 2–3 suggestions for the chosen goal (`suggestionsFor()`, `WK_TPL`), each with a reason from the schedule (`whyFor()`; hard sessions within 2 days of a meet or 1 day after one are flagged, listed last).
- Templates are established high school XC sessions with effort parts only (`P_()`): Daniels VDOT easy / threshold / interval / repetition, Tom Schwartz CV, 5K and mile pace (from VDOT). `PACE_SYS` gives each its standard name ("Interval (I), Daniels VDOT").
- Flow: Use this → `tplSheet(t)` (parts by pace name; steppers for reps and rest) → Choose runners → `wkFromTpl()` saves (or reuses) an ordinary workout → `openWorkoutFlow({workoutId})` (picker bar; step 2 lists each runner's target and basis, `targetsList()`) → Start. Runners with no race this season are flagged with **Time trial** (`timeTrialSheet()`: Mile / 3200m / 5K + time → a dated hand-timed typed result "Time trial", which `paceProfile()` uses like a race) or **Run with a group** (the group maker; they run on their group's middle runner).
- **Rollback-safe:** a saved suggestion is a plain workout (no new top-level fields: the workouts rules only allow name/reps/rest/restUnit/segments); its template id rides inside each part as `tpl`. Older versions read it as an effort workout.
- Tests: `tests/e2e24.js`; `tests/tools/today.js [date] [label]` screenshots the tab.

## Effort parts without a time (2.13)
- **Effort parts without a time** (`segEffort(s)`): a part with an effort (Fast/Tempo/CV/Race pace/Easy/Jog) and a distance but no time is effort-based like "By effort": `EFF_REF` maps CV → cv, Tempo → threshold, Fast → interval, Race pace → 5k, Easy/Jog → easy (`segRef()`). Everything that asked `s.mode==='effort'` asks `segEffort(s)`.

## Groups in the workout flow (2.12)
- **Groups** (`openWorkoutFlow()`, step 2 "Make groups"): `suggestGroups()` sorts runners by their pace for the workout's first effort part (`paceOfFor()`, 5K pace when none) and starts a new group when a runner is more than 2.9% slower than the group's fastest (so the 3% spread warning never fires); runners with no pace yet go last together. Each runner has a group picker (or New group); groups renumber. Group stopwatches are named "Group 1"…, singletons by the runner.

## Suggested training paces (2.11)
- **Pace math:** Jack Daniels' VDOT formulas (`vdotOf()`, `velAt()`, `raceTimeAt()`, `pacesFor()`):
  - easy 62–70% of VDOT, threshold 88%, interval 97.5%, repetition 105%
  - CV = the pace for about 30 minutes; 5K and mile = predicted race times
  - `tests/e2e20.js` checks them against Daniels' tables (VDOT 50 and 60, within 1–3 s).
- **`paceProfile(a)`:** the runner's best performance by VDOT this season (any result 1500m or longer: hand-timed, official, or a dated typed result), else a race in the last 120 days. Never an undated or old PR, and Injury/Illness-tagged results are left out. Shows a basis line ("Based on 5K season best 18:32.6, 9/19").
- **Runner card:** "Suggested training paces" (`paceTableHTML()`): every effort per 100, 200, 400, 800, 1000 and mile, labeled estimates.
- **Workouts:** a part's "Pace given as: By effort" (CV, threshold, interval, repetition, 5K pace, mile pace, easy, custom % of 5K speed). Each stopwatch gets its runner's own targets.
  - Group stopwatches use the middle runner and warn when the group's 5K paces differ by more than 3% (card, Targets sheet).
- **⋯ > Targets…** (`targetsSheet()`): shows the targets and adjusts them in 2 s/mile steps. Remembered in `S.paceAdj['<sorted runner ids>|<workoutId>']` (this phone).
