# Official results, import and merging runners

Importing the coach's history file, official results, pasting official results into a race, matching and merging runners. No fetching from results sites (rule 12): results come in only through the history file or a paste.

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-29 with its wording unchanged; the version tags in headings say when each part was built (the full old file is `docs/archive/CLAUDE-before-split.md`, and version-by-version history is in REQUESTS.md). "See X" in the text names a section heading; the index in CLAUDE.md says which file holds each one. See also: `docs/data-and-charts.md`, `docs/race-mode.md`.

## Official results and career history (2.9)
- **Import** (admin only in a team; any phone without one): Settings > Import history file, or Results > Import history. File format `mustang-splits-history/v1` (`{format, generated, source, notes, athletes:[{name, aliases, school, results:[{season, grade, level HS|MS, distance, distance_m, date, time, time_s, place, flag, meet, division, meet_name_cut_off, possible_duplicate_of}]}]}`). These files hold minors' full names: `*.history.json` and `data/` are git-ignored, and the repo is public. Tests use `tests/fixtures/fake-history-fixture.json` (fake names).
- `importPlan()` (nothing saved) → `previewSheet()` → `saveImport()`. Runner matching `rosterCands()`: full name 4, first + initial 3, nickname (`NICK` groups, e.g. Benjamin = Ben, Isabella = Izzy) 2, first only 1; certain = one candidate scoring 3+, else the coach confirms; aliases count as names; gender must agree when both are known. A confirmed match is remembered as `matches[nameHash] = athleteId` (`nameHash()` = SHA-256 of the normalized full name, salted); new runners get their full name (`storeName()`, 2.11.1). Missing runners are offered as new unless graduated (grade projected from the last result).
- Meet names: `meetKey()` normalizes (years, "44th", "Annual", Invitational → Invite, cut-off names by prefix); `MEET_ALIASES` maps every known variant to the 2026 series ids with official names; unknown names that share all their words group together (`groupMeetNames()`); middle school meets never fold into a high school series. The coach can change any series in the preview. Past meets: id `mh-<date>-<series>`, the series' current course (`KNOWN_COURSE`), levels from divisions; MS results get no meet.
- This season's series got their official names by a `migrate()` rename keyed by id (`SERIES_RENAME`; only if still the 2.7 seed name). The seed keeps its 2.7 ids.
- Duplicates: entries with `possible_duplicate_of` on the same day, distance and within 2 s: keep the one with a division. Every result has a key `nameHash|date|meters|time`, so a re-import adds nothing.
- Storage: one record per import part (`OFF_PART` = 600 results) in IndexedDB `official` (DB version 2) and in a team `teams/{t}/official/{id}`. Results: `{k, aid|null, name (short), g, season, grade, level, dist, date, t, place, meet, seriesId, meetId, courseId, division}` (division `GV`… or `GMS`/`BMS`, '' if the level is unknown). Snapshot before saving. Undo / Remove = soft delete of the whole import plus the runners, series, courses and meets it created (`undoImport()`, trash kind `import`, restore `restoreImport()`). Delete permanently scrubs official results (`scrubOfficialDoc()`, and sync.js `purgeRunner`). Not in backup files (re-import the file instead).
- **Views:** `offEntries()` turns official results into read-only race models (`h.official`, one per day, meet, division and distance; memoized by `offGen`), so `allRaces()` (HS; `allRaces(true)` adds MS), `pastRaces()` (goals), `courseFactors()` and the team view use them. `resultsFor()` merges an official and a hand-timed result for the same runner, day and distance into one (official time, `x.hand` beside it). Course factors count a runner once per day (official preferred). PR = `careerBest()` (PR list or fastest official result; hand-timed races don't override a typed PR there; the file's PR flags are ignored); `stampBests()` and Compare to PR use it. `memoize()` caches heavy view data for one render.
- Results > Meets: official lists sit in their season and meet with an Official badge and the hand-timed column when the team timed it too (`officialHTML()`); middle school in its own group; on a phone without a team the section is titled "Official results".
- Runner card career (`careerHTML()`, high school 5K by default): 5K PR progression (`stepChart()` step line), season by season with grade, the same meet year over year (by series), early / mid / late season arc (`arcOf()`: to Sep 10, Sep 11–Oct 5, Oct 6 on; best pace per mile), official vs hand-timed, and middle school in its own section.
- Team view: a season picker, Girls and Boys top-5 average and 1–5 spread per meet (5K), official results when they exist for that day and division (else hand-timed), a season-by-season table.
- 2.9.1:
  - **Backups** carry `official` (all records) and a restore (or a snapshot) merges them in without ever removing (`mergeOfficial()`).
  - **Sectional/State** (`PLACE_SERIES`): the course comes from the place in the printed name (`placeOf()`: "WIAA D2 Sectional - Kiel", "Sectional 4 - Waupaca", "@ Wisconsin Rapids"; `courseByPlace()` finds "Kiel HS" or creates "New London"). `fixPlaceCourses()` moves meets that 2.9.0 imported (`mh-` ids only) on an admin / no-team phone. Official models take the course from their meet.
  - **Varsity/JV:** `offLevel()` = a coach's level edit (append-only `{op:'level', k, level}` in the record's `edits`, newest wins, '' clears; admins since 2.9.1, any member since 3.24), else the file's division, else the schedule (`levelGuess()`: a this-season meet in the series with one level). **3.24:** everything the file doesn't label JV counts as Varsity for the awards (`rowLevel()`, docs/season-and-awards.md); the JV labels `parseDiv()` reads ("Junior", "Junior V", "Junior Varsi", "Jr", "JV", frosh/soph, open, reserve, novice…) make the result JV with the source "imported label". `parseDiv()` reads more forms (Frosh/Soph, open, reserve = JV; championship, gold = V).
  - `levelSheet()` (the 2.9.1 admin sheet) still exists; since 3.24 every official list in Data › Meets has **Edit Varsity/JV** (`jvEditSheet()`, any coach) instead. Re-importing a file adds levels to results already imported (`plan.levelFix`). Rules: admin `appended('edits')` on `official`; 3.24: a member may append a level edit (`levelAppended()`: the newest entry is `op 'level'`, V / JV / '', no other fields).
- Tests: `tests/e2e15.js` (importer, re-import, Undo/restore, team sync, purge, 600-result performance), `tests/e2e16.js` (a real 2.8.1 phone and a 2.9.0 phone together under the current rules; offline).

## Merge runners and Girls/Boys (2.9.2)
- **Matcher** (`rosterCands()`): a candidate needs the same first name (or a `NICK` nickname). Last name scores:
  - 4 = full last name matches
  - 3 = an abbreviation that begins the file's last name ("Ben To." for T.)
  - 2 = same initial only, or any nickname match
  - 1 = the roster has no last name

  A Girls/Boys difference never hides a candidate (it's shown; 2.9.0 hid "Ben T." because of a stray "Girls" label and created "Ben To."). Automatic only when remembered (through merges, `mergedTo()`) or one full-name match with no other candidate. Every other plausible match is asked, and Save stays disabled ("Confirm n matches first", or "Confirm all suggested matches").
- **Girls/Boys:** `offGender()` = the linked runner's Team tab setting (never a guess, never a default to Girls). Results not linked to a runner keep the label printed in the file. No setting = "Unassigned" (official list with Girls/Boys buttons, `unassignedHTML()`; a Team view note). The file's gender only seeds a new runner when every label agrees. A level printed without Girls/Boys is kept as `r.lv`. Everything is derived when shown, so a merge or a gender change re-sorts at once.
- **Merge runners** (admin / no team; Team tab): `mergeSheet()` → `doMerge(dup, real)`.
  - Takes a snapshot first, then adds a merge record `S.merges` (`{id: dup, to, at, byName}`, synced kind `merges`, rules admin-only, soft delete).
  - Saved results are never rewritten: `mapModel()` (in `allRaces()` and `pastRaces()`) and `offEntries()` map the duplicate's id (rows, marks, tags; both in one race → the faster time).
  - Moved for real: this phone's stopwatch links and the live race (`mergeLocal()`, marks as append-only versions; other phones do the same when the record arrives), and PRs (faster per distance). The real runner gets the duplicate's Girls/Boys only if it had none.
  - The duplicate goes to the trash (kind `merge`). Undo / Restore (`undoMerge()`) reverses all of it.
  - Remembered import matches follow merges.

## Data organization: levels and re-import (2.11.1)
- **Real data never goes in the repo** (the repo is public): no backups, history files or screenshots with real names. Tests use fake names. (The first name + last initial rule is gone: runners have full names.)
- **Official levels** (`offLevel()`): a coach's level edit, else the file's label, else the level of that runner's hand-timed race that day (`handLevels()`), else Sectional/State = Varsity. Otherwise none here; since 3.24 "none" counts as Varsity for the awards (`rowLevel()`), so Data health no longer lists unlabeled results.
  - A label printed for the other gender is ignored. This was the Ben bug: the file labeled his 2026 results "Girls Varsity", so after the merge they became "Boys Varsity" while his unlabeled teammates were plain "Boys", leaving him in a one-runner race at each meet.
  - `parseDiv()` reads cut-off labels ("Junior", "Junior V", "Junior Varsi", "Jr" = JV; "Varsity -", "Varsity D2/3", "Varsi" = V).
- **Re-import updates** (`planUpdates()`): Varsity/JV levels from the file, runner links (`{op:'link', k, aid}` edits on the import record, applied in `offEntries()`), and full names for roster runners whose name is a short form (`fullNameFor()`: "Ben T." → "Ben Tollefsrud", a made-up example).
  - Previewed with counts, applied together with one Undo, and nothing is ever added twice.

## Name matching (2.12)
- **Name matching:** `splitName()` reads a hyphenated last name as one name ("Ivy D." = Ivy Delacroix-Moss). `normName()` is unchanged (it feeds `nameHash()`; changing it would break remembered matches).

## Official results importer (3.21; was Paste official results, 3.12)
- **Where:** the same places as 3.12 (a finished race on the race screen, `[data-ra=pasteoff]`; Team history and Races on this phone, `[data-hpaste]`; each hand-timed race inside a meet, `divRaceHTML()`), the button now says **Official results**. Also Settings > Data > **Import history file**: a file that isn't the history JSON (CSV, TSV, text, .xlsx, PDF) opens `poRacePicker(file)`, which asks which saved race it belongs to and opens the importer with the file read (`#histFile` accepts those types since 3.21).
- **Input** (app.js "official results from a results page or a file"): the text box (paste, or the Paste button), or **Choose a file** (`#poFile`, `PO_ACCEPT`). `poLoadFile(file)`: bytes are decoded by `poDecode()` (a UTF-8 or UTF-16 BOM, UTF-16 without one when every other byte is zero, UTF-8, else Windows-1252: the timing company's sectional CSV is UTF-16LE with a BOM); a zip is read as Excel (`poXlsx()`: the sheet with the most rows, shared and inline strings, a number between 0.002 and 0.07 days is a time); `%PDF` is read by `poPdf()` (Flate streams inflated with `DecompressionStream`, `Tf/Td/TD/Tm/T*/TL/Tj/TJ/'/"` followed, literal and hex strings, ToUnicode `bfchar`/`bfrange` maps, glyph runs grouped into lines by y and cells by gaps; a scan has no text layer and says so). The note under the buttons says what was loaded ("Export.csv: CSV file (UTF-16), 105 lines").
- **Parsing** `parseOfficialText(text, school)` → `{rows (ours), all, skipped, lines, read:{format, cols, header, byName}}`: `poKind()` picks tab-separated, CSV (comma or semicolon, quotes honoured by `poCsv()`) or lines. A table goes through `poTable()`: a header row is found by words (`PO_HEAD`: place / bib / name / time / school / grade, and columns to skip such as pace, points, gender, age group, state qualifier, splits); the rest of the columns by their contents (`poColumns()`: the time column is the one with the most 4–90-minute times, the largest when several; the name column has two-word values that are nearly all different; the school column has our school in it, or repeats; place counts up; grade is 9–12 / Fr–Sr; bib is a big number). Each row's finish is the longest time in its time cell (`poFinOf()`, never a pace: `PO_TIME` refuses "/mi", "min/m"); rows without one are listed under "Lines without a usable time or name" with why ("did not finish or start" for DNF / Not started). Lines of text go through `poLine()` one by one (the 3.12 rules: the finish is the longest time on the line; place, grade, the name as the longest run of words in a cell, "Last, First" turned around; a first and a last name needed), for every line, with `ours` set by the school. **One cell per line** (a phone's copy): `poRegroup()` finds the period as the distance at which the most place lines step up by one (bibs, team points, overall positions and splits don't), takes the first of the columns that count up as the place, reads the lines before the first record as a header when they hold header words, and checks that in most records the name comes before the time; else it splits at the next place in order once the last record has its time (a "1" again means the next race on the page only after a whole record). Groups are read as a table, else each as a line.
- **School:** `poSchoolRe(school)`: "Little Chute", "Little Chute HS / High School", any capitals, and the initials in capitals ("LC", "LCHS"; never in a name). `S.settings.school` stays the setting (default `PASTE_SCHOOL`). A list with **no school anywhere** (a dual meet's list) falls back to roster names: rows whose name matches a runner (`poCands()`, score 3+) are ours but each must be confirmed (`byName`).
- **Matching** `matchOfficial(rows, M)`: as 3.12 (the race's runners first, then the roster; sure = one race runner scoring 3+ with no close rival; the other team's race skipped and explained; anyone not in the race, two lines on one runner, confirmed), plus `poCands()`: `rosterCands()` and then an edit distance of 1 (2 on long last names) on each name, or a first name that starts the other ("Bea" / "Beatrice"), as plausible matches that are never sure ("Close spelling, check this match").
- **Never silent:** the sheet shows **What was read** (`.po-read`: the file note, the format, lines → results with a time and without, the columns found and not found, how many rows are ours and what matched them, how many are from other schools), **Rows from other schools** (each with a select to claim it for a runner: `[data-po-add]`, then flagged to confirm; opened by itself when nothing of ours was found) and **Lines without a usable time or name**. Nothing of ours found: a warning with the counts and the other rows to pick from, instead of only "No results found".
- **Preview and Save** unchanged from 3.12 (hand time and difference beside each official time, who has no official time, Save disabled until every uncertain match is confirmed; `saveOfficialPaste()`: a snapshot, chosen `src 'official'` marks with the place, one Undo). Official cells are editable since 3.21 (their source can be changed in the time editor; see docs/data-safety.md).
- Fixtures: `tests/tools/po-fixtures.js` (fake names), modeled on the real files: the timing company's UTF-16 CSV (Pos, Race No, Name, Time, Age Group, Team Points, Gender, Team, Pos In Team, State Qualifier, Pace, One Mile, O/Pos, Two Mile, O/Pos; "Not started" rows), the same page one cell per line, athletic.net tabs with team scores and both races, MileSplit single spaces with a pace column, Hy-Tek fixed width, a stored-zip .xlsx with times as day fractions, PDFs with plain and hex (ToUnicode) strings. Tests: `tests/e2e50.js` section 3; `tests/e2e41.js` still covers the 3.12 save path. Hooks: `MSApp.poParse(text, school)`, `poFile(file)`, `poDecode(buf)`, `poXlsx(buf)`, `poPdf(buf)`, `poMatch(rows, entryId)`.
- **Why the coach's two attempts failed (diagnosed 2026-11-02):** the CSV downloaded from the results site (`Export.csv`, four identical copies in iCloud Downloads, 10/23) is UTF-16LE with a BOM; the history-file picker accepted only `.json` (the Files picker greys the CSV out, and a forced pick said "That file isn't JSON"); pasted into the 3.12 box, commas were not cell separators, so a line's name run became "52,1795,Name,…" and the "Last, First" rule turned it into digits (no name, nothing found). The page paste itself wasn't on the Mac (`text.txt` in the data folder holds only the words "Mustang Splits backup"): the probable cause is the one-cell-per-line copy, where 3.12 started a new record at any bare number once a time was seen, so "Team Points" split each runner from "Little Chute". 3.21 reads all of these (e2e50).

## Paste official results (3.12)
- **Paste official results** (app.js "official results pasted from a results page (3.12)", before End race): a button on a finished race (race screen, `[data-ra=pasteoff]`, the saved copy via `savedSrc()`), on Team history races and Races on this phone (`[data-hpaste]` + `data-hwhere`), and on each hand-timed race inside a meet (`divRaceHTML()`). `officialPasteSheet(src)`: school name (`S.settings.school`, default `PASTE_SCHOOL` 'Little Chute'), a text box (and Paste via the clipboard), Read results → `parseOfficialText(text, school)` → `matchOfficial(rows, M)` → preview → `saveOfficialPaste(src, picks)`.
  - **Parser:** one result per line (`poLine()`): only lines naming the school (plus "HS", "High School"); the finish is the longest time on the line (never a pace, split or "+gap"); place = a leading number, grade 9–12 / Fr–Sr; the name is the longest run of words in one cell (tabs, 2+ spaces or "|" split cells), "Last, First" turned around; at least a first and a last name (team-score lines never count). A phone layout that copies one cell per line is regrouped (a new record starts at a place line once the last record had its time). Tested formats: tab-separated tables (athletic.net style), Hy-Tek fixed width, MileSplit-style single spaces with a pace column, one cell per line.
  - **Matching:** `rosterCands()` against the race's runners first, then the roster: sure = one race runner scoring 3+ with no close rival; a runner of the other team who isn't in this race (the page had the boys' race too) is skipped by default and says why; anyone not in the race, any weaker match and two lines on one runner must be confirmed (Save says "Confirm n matches first"). The preview shows each official time with the hand time and difference, and who has no official time (their hand time stays).
  - **Saving** (append-only, through `applyVersions()`, so a team race goes out with `SYNC.appendHistoryEdits` and a local one with `logPut`): a snapshot first ("Before pasting official results"); per runner a new chosen mark at the finish `{mid:'po-<imp>-<rid>', ci, rid, t, chosen:true, src:'official', place, imp}` (any other chosen mark there gets a `chosen:false` version), `{op:'row', rid, name, start?}` for a runner who wasn't hand-timed, and `{op:'official', imp, n, src:'pasted'}`. One Undo appends the reverse (`deleted` versions, `rowUndo`, `officialUndo`). No rules change (history edits only need the caller's uid).
  - **Reading it:** `modelOf()` keeps `src`/`place` on marks, applies `row`/`rowUndo`, sets `M.offPaste`, and skips any op it doesn't know (older ones made a phantom mark). `counts()` prefers a chosen official mark. `withCells()` marks that cell `off` with `handT` (the earliest hand-timed mark) and `oplace`, so the hand time shows beside it, the hand-timed splits stay and the last split runs to the official finish; official cells aren't editable (toast says Undo or paste again; Edit times shows them read-only). The race gets the Official badge in Data > Meets; `combineDiv()` keeps the hand time when an imported official list lands on a pasted one. Versions older than 3.12 see the official time as a chosen correction (rollback-safe).
  - `savedSrc(r)` (3.12): a finished race that another coach saved finds its Team history copy (`teamHistory`/`teamRaces` by `race.raceId`), so corrections and pasted official results show on every phone's race screen.
- Tests: `tests/e2e41.js` (Highlights cards, paste parser/matching/save/Undo, team path); e2e36 and e2e38 read the new cards; `tests/tools/fake-season.js` gives one boy exactly even splits.
