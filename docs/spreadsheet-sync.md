# The live coach's spreadsheet (3.20)

Settings › Data › **Spreadsheet**: a one-way mirror of the app's data the coach can read anywhere. Two targets, either or both: a **Google Sheet** in the coach's Drive kept up to date by the team's Firebase Cloud Function (`functions/`; set-up steps in `docs/sheet-sync-setup.md`), and an **Excel copy** the app makes on the phone. All in app.js "live coach's spreadsheet (3.20)" (after the 3.19 section), sync.js "live spreadsheet" (sheetState, sheetWrite, the two listeners), firestore.rules `sheetStateOk()` / `sheetContentOk()` and `match /sheet/{docId}`, and `functions/index.js` + `functions/mirror.js`. See also `docs/banquet-and-exports.md` (the eight workbook sheets, `wbSheets()`), `docs/season-and-awards.md` (awards logic), `docs/data-and-charts.md` (latest meet, growth focus, ratings), `docs/training-and-paces.md` (pace groups).

## Principles
- **The app is the source of truth.** The spreadsheet never edits app data; nothing is read back except the coach's notes column (Google only), and that only to put it back.
- **Coaches only.** The Coach's Brief names next steps, readiness and award standings, so the Google Sheet is private to the coach's account (shared only with the function) and the Settings page says to share it only with coaches. The eight data sheets stay free of coach-only content (e2e49 checks every cell of every data sheet against the coach-only word list; the real backup too).
- **Only what changed is written.** Each sheet is hashed on the phone; a phone sends only sheets whose hash differs from what it sent before and from what the function reports as applied; the function rewrites only sheets whose hash changed. Sheet order, column widths and the charts are set once, when a sheet is first made. The coach's notes column is kept by row key.
- **Rate limit:** at most one push a minute from a phone (`sheetSoon()`: the first change waits 1.5 s so a burst coalesces, later ones until a minute after the last push), and the function waits until a minute after its last sync, then 4 s for the rest of a push to land. **Sync now** pushes at once (the function still keeps its minute).
- **Rollback-safe:** new settings (`S.settings.sheetXlsx`, `sheetSent`, `sheetLast`, `sheetXlsxAt`, `sheetXlsxSaved`), a new Firestore collection (`sheet`) and a tiny IndexedDB database `mustang-splits-files` (the chosen Excel file's handle, desktop browsers only). Older versions ignore all of it; rules 3.20.0 only add the collection, so they are published first.

## The sheets
`sheetContent()` → `{season, sheets:[...]}` for the **current** season (`seasonOf(Date.now())`, not the one picked on the Data tab: the live sheet is this season's). Each sheet: `{key, name, order, rows, keys, cols, freeze, filter, cf, merges, heights, tab, chart, spark, styles, dxfs, hash}`; rows and cells as `xlsx.js` takes them (`null | string | number | {v, s, f}`), `keys[i]` the row's key or null (header, blank and note rows), `hash` a 16-hex-character FNV hash of the rows, keys, layout and chart.
1. **Coach's Brief** (`briefSheet(Y)`, key `brief`): "what should I do this week?" in five boxes, each with a title row and a *What this means* line:
   - **Your notes**: six keyed rows (`note|1…6`) for the coach's own lines (typed in the notes column).
   - **1. Celebrate**: the runners with a new PR or season best at the last meet (`latestMeet()` stories with a `pr` or `sb` highlight): what, time, faster by, meet. Keys `cel|<runner>`.
   - **2. Watch**: `growthFocus()` (a pattern over 3+ rated, untagged races this season, course- and weather-adjusted, slower than season-best pace): the pattern (`GF_WHY`), the recent adjusted paces, the target, the first suggested workout (the app's Next step logic) and the check-in line. Keys `watch|<runner>`.
   - **3. Pace groups**: Girls and Boys grouped by CV pace with `groupByPace()` (within 3%, fastest first, the same groups as Training › Team training paces at CV), each runner's CV, interval and 5K race pace per mile from `paceProfile()`, the basis; runners with no pace at the end. Keys `pg|<runner>`.
   - **4. Championship readiness** (`readyRows(Y)`): per runner, the last three races and the season average as 5K-equivalent times on an average race day (`adjPace` where the day is rated, raw pace otherwise; typed and Injury/Illness-tagged races left out), last vs average in seconds, and a plain note: *Peaking* (last race the season's best), *Trending up* (last race ≥1% faster than the average), *Steady* (within 1%), *Coming back* (each of the last three faster than the one before), *Building* (a little off the average), *Early days* (under three races). Keys `ready|<runner>`.
   - **5. Awards tracker**: from `awData(Y, team)` for each team in `awTeams(Y)`: MVR standings with the math (varsity races, places, sum, average, standing or "needs n of total"), Varsity Letter progress (varsity races of total, majority, championship meets run, senior with 3–4 seasons), Rookie of the Year candidates (first high-school season: varsity races, improvement). **Blank with a note until varsity participation is confirmed**: when any race day has runners whose level is unknown (`awData().unknown`), the box is one line saying how many days to set in Data › Highlights › Season › Awards. Keys `mvr|<team>|<runner>`, `let|…`, `roty|…`.
   - A last line says the Brief is never shared and the data sheets can be.
2. **Season board … Raw data**: `wbSheets(Y)` from 3.19, unchanged in content; each data row now carries a key (`kr()`): `r|<runner id>` (Season board, Meet grid, Progress, Training paces with `|<effort>`), `sp|<date>|<runner>|<checkpoint>|<source>` (Splits), `d|<date>` and `total` (Team), `d|<date>` (Weather), `x|<date>|<runner>|<distance>|<source>|<entry>` (Raw data). The Excel export (Data › Highlights › Share › Coach's workbook) doesn't show the keys.
3. **Charts** (`shChart(key, sheet, Y)`, from the sheet's own rows): a title that says what to look for, categories, series with team colours (Girls `#b8247f`, Boys `#0a6e94`), values in minutes (times) or seconds (spreads, PR drops). Season board: *Team ladder* (bar, season best per runner); Meet grid: *Season trend by runner* (line, one series per runner); Splits: pace per mile by segment at the last meet with splits; Progress: *Year over year* (line per runner by season); Team: *Top-5 average and 1–5 spread over the season* ("lower is better; the pack is tightening when the spread line falls"; spreads on the right axis); Training paces: CV pace per runner; Weather: temperature and wind per meet; Raw data: *PR drops this season*.

## The Google Sheet (team + function)
- **Firestore** `teams/{t}/sheet/`:
  - `state` (phones write, `SYNC.sheetState(patch)`): `{sheetId, hello, off, requestedAt, sheets, byName, updatedAt, updatedBy}`. `hello: true` asks the function for its address; `sheetId` connects a sheet (from the pasted link); `off: true` disconnects; `requestedAt` (server time) after a push.
  - `c-<key>` and `c-<key>-<part>` (phones write, `SYNC.sheetWrite(docs)`): `{key, name, hash, part, parts, season, data}`; `data` is the sheet's JSON (`shDoc()`), split into parts of at most 800,000 characters (Firestore's 1 MB document). The real team's largest sheet (Raw data, 386 results) is about 92 KB.
  - `status` (the function only; rules refuse every client write): `{status: 'needs-sheet'|'needs-share'|'ok'|'error'|'off', saEmail, error, note, lastSyncAt, applied:{key: hash}, charts:{key: chartId}, sheetIds, title, url, changed, runs, lockAt}`.
  - sync.js listens to `state` and `status` (`MSApp.sheetRemote(kind, doc)`); permission-denied (rules before 3.20.0) → `quietKinds` `sheet`, and the page says the rules need publishing.
- **Pushing** (`sheetTouch(why)` → `sheetSoon()` → `sheetPush()`): the triggers are wired at the save points: `logRace` (a race saved), `applyVersions` (a correction; `official` when the versions came from Paste official results), `tagEdit` (a tag), `saveImport` (a history import), `setLinks`/`setLevels` (official-result edits), roster changes (`addRunnerSheet`, the roster paste, `resultSheet` name/gender, `removeRunner`, Recently deleted restore, Data health's add, a merge), schedule changes (`saveScheduleImport`, `meetEditSheet`, `newSeason`, `setAttending`, restore), `awPut` (awards), and `sheetDaily()` (at boot and hourly: a push when the last one is over 20 hours old, so date-relative text refreshes). `MSApp.sheet.log()` counts them (tests). A phone pushes only when the team has a sheet connected or the Excel copy is on; otherwise the change is remembered (`SH.dirty`) and goes with the first push after connecting.
- **The function** (`functions/index.js`): `sheetSync` runs on any write under `teams/{t}/sheet` (not its own `status`); `sheetDaily` at 5:30 Central for every connected team. One run at a time per team (a `lockAt` lease in `status`, 8 minutes at most); a run waits for the minute, settles 4 s, assembles the content (`mirror.assemble`: parts joined, incomplete sets skipped, Brief first by `order`), mirrors it (`mirror.sync`), reports, and goes again if `state.requestedAt` moved while it worked. `hello` without a sheet → `needs-sheet` with `saEmail`; a 403/404 from Google → `needs-share` with what to do; anything else → `error` with Google's message. The function's address comes from its credentials (`client_email`) or the metadata server.
- **The mirror** (`functions/mirror.js`, pure, injected API `{get, batchUpdate, values}`): makes missing sheets in the app's order (and a hidden *Chart data* sheet; Google's empty *Sheet1* is hidden), then for each sheet whose hash changed: reads the grid once and keeps the notes by key (`notesFrom`), clears values/formats/merges/filter/conditional formats, writes the grid with `updateCells` in 400-row chunks (`gridRows`: the title and *Updated from Mustang Splits …* line, 12 rows reserved for the chart on data sheets and 1 on the Brief, column A = key (hidden), content from column B, the yellow **Coach notes** column last with the kept notes), formulas and ranges shifted into place (`shiftFormula`, `gridRange`), frozen panes below the chart rows, the filter, colour scales and formula rules, row heights, `=SPARKLINE()` where the Excel sparkline was, the chart's numbers in its block of the Chart data sheet (100 rows per sheet) and the chart itself (`addChart` once at B3, 820 × 196 px; `updateChartSpec` after that, so widening or moving it by hand stays). Column widths, the hidden key column and the notes column width are set only when the sheet is made. A note on a row that disappears is dropped.
- **Settings page** (`sheetSettings()` / `sheetSetUI()`): the Google Sheet group (Not set up → Set up; Waiting for the sync function (with what to do if it never answers); the address with Copy, the three steps and the link field → Connect; Connected: title, last synced, Open the sheet, Sync now, Disconnect (a confirm; the sheet stays); Can't open the sheet yet with the address; Sync failed with Google's message), the coaches-only reminder, the Excel copy group, and a line listing the triggers. The Google group needs the phone on the team.

## The Excel copy ("Keep an Excel copy")
`S.settings.sheetXlsx`: after every push the phone rebuilds the same nine sheets as an `.xlsx` (`sheetXlsxBlob()`: `MSXlsx.build` with a *Coach notes* column added to every sheet, empty; the Brief first) and keeps it in memory (`SH.xlsx`). On a desktop browser with `showSaveFilePicker` (Chrome, Edge), **Choose a file to keep updated** picks a file once (its handle in the `mustang-splits-files` database) and every later copy is written into it (after the browser's permission prompt once per session). On iPhone, iOS has no way for a web app to rewrite a file without the coach, so **Save the latest copy** opens the share sheet (Save to Files); the page shows *Updated* and *Saved to Files* times so it's clear when the saved copy is behind. Notes typed in Excel are never read back (a known limit; the Google Sheet is the one that keeps notes).

## What is not synced, and known limits
- The Google Sheet shows the current season only (the Excel export in Data › Highlights › Share takes any season).
- Frozen rows on a data sheet include the 12 chart rows (the header stays under the chart); on a phone that is a lot of frozen screen, so the Google Sheets app is best in landscape or the data sheets' rows unfrozen there (View › Freeze) — the app itself is the better phone view.
- A sheet the coach renames in Google is seen as missing and made again under the app's name (the renamed one keeps its contents and stops updating); the same for a deleted sheet. Deleting the *Chart data* sheet makes the charts lose their numbers until the next change of each sheet.
- The Awards tracker is blank until every race day's level is known (by design).
